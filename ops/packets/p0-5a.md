Nothing is falsified at C_lo. Open, falsified at no class, are the condensed phases and the Al phosphates. Ca2P2O7 (crystalline; its amorphous row undetermined), Ca(PO3)2 glass and crystal, and the Li polyphosphate baseline are inconclusive at every class and never pass. AlPO4, Al(PO3)3, the Al-polyphosphate gel and the baked aluminophosphate baseline are undetermined (no Al(PO3)3 thermochemistry, no bound on the exchange entropy of the Al perchlorate sink). The Ca orthophosphates proceed on C_lo, where they are not falsified. It changes only if data of form (A) or (B) arrive and the computed E_lb of a condensed phase clears the top of C_mid plus its margin. This section reports the condition; the headline is the operator's decision.
