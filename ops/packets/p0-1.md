## Purpose

This is the G0 science audit of the charter that the preprint stands on. The audit covers:

- the claim register: claims, observables and the kill-criterion structure;
- the regimes: their inputs and the ranges that are set or still null;
- the outcome branches.

The preprint is a thermodynamic screening paper; its working title is in `ops/tracks/p0.yaml`. It inherits the charter's physical chain, regimes and scope limits. A charter error therefore propagates into every P0 lane, and the audit is due before the day 3 sessions that require cp1 (P0-W2.3, P0-W4.3, P0-W5.3, P0-W8.3).

## Charter items P0 relies on

1. **The physical chain C1 to C5** (`charter/claim_register.yaml`). The links are:
   - C1 Anchoring (anchor);
   - C2 Sequestration (sequester);
   - C3 Nucleation, the crux (nucleate);
   - C4 Conformality (outcome condition);
   - C5 Thermodynamic stability (outcome condition).

   P0 screens the governing parameters of this chain and states the precision a first-principles validation would need. It uses each claim's decisive observable and its threshold status. Every threshold is pending G2; the deck's C3 bands are recorded only as a proposal.
2. **The R-slurry and R-cell input ranges** (`charter/regimes.yaml`). P0 takes from this file:
   - the input list of each regime;
   - the three non-null bounds: R-slurry pH min, R-slurry water-activity max, and R-cell potential min (from the deck, with no reference electrode stated);
   - the null ranges, which P0 must treat as open rather than fill in;
   - the regime assignment of each claim.

   Every input is provisional, pending S12.
3. **The branches** (`charter/branches.md`): what is published under feasible, inconclusive and falsified; the invariants across branches; the two modifiers. P0 must be written so that it is valid on every branch.
4. **The deletion register** (`charter/deletion_register.yaml`): the five controls, and the rule that the deletion test must degrade the verdict through C2 to C4 because C5 survives deletion unchanged. P0's screen has to respect this when it ranks parameters.
5. **The scope guard** (`charter/scope_guard.md`): the does-not-claim list, the insulating-film thickness limitation and the forbidden-claim patterns SG01 to SG14. P0 text and captions are checked against them.
