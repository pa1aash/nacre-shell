### The change

- **`src/nacre/structures/compare.py`.** Regeneration compares coordinates within **1e-2 Å** (minimum image in periodic directions), which is physically negligible for illustrative drawings (bonds are tens of times longer). Atom count, species and atom order are exact, as are per-atom string arrays, periodicity and provenance metadata (builder, parameters, seed, library versions, source ids, charge, label and every other non-float value). Four geometry-derived float keys (`c_hex`, `radius_initial`, `radius_final`, `ff_energy_kcal_mol`) are compared to a relative 1e-3. The comparison lists every difference in every file, not only the first.
- **Slab order.** `slab.stable_order` sorts by element, then z, y and x clusters (gaps up to 0.05 Å count as equal), then input order. A test perturbs positions by 1e-12, 1e-6 and 1e-3 Å and checks that the order holds.
- **Oligomer conformer record.** `structures/preprint/sources/oligomer_conformers.json` keeps the conformer, keyed by parameters and seed, with the platform it was generated on (Darwin arm64). It reproduces the committed conformer exactly (0.0 Å). `oligomer.build` reads the record; `python -m nacre.structures.oligomer --generate` regenerates it.

## Task C: hero draft (Fig 1)

### Sourced Ca²⁺ hydration geometry

- **Source.** `python -m nacre.structures.evidence --fetch` read `origin/preprint/p0-w1:lit/evidence_table.csv` at commit `77806d8080e9b7bc6c58b9e1266811b3d01b4109` (blob `a9abe45b…`). It keeps the `ca_hydration_number` rows verbatim in `structures/preprint/sources/ca_hydration_w1.json`.
- **Rule.** Take the best-grade, full-text, numeric row. The coordination number is its value rounded to an integer. The Ca–O distance is the Ca–Ow distance printed in the same row. Experimental ranges among the context rows must contain the drawn coordination number.
- **Result: row TE-072** (SCAN-ML MD, grade B, full text) gives **coordination number 7** (6.85) and **Ca–O 2.37 Å**. TE-073 (experimental 6–10) and TE-074 (EXAFS CN 8, Ca–O 2.46 Å, grade D) are context.
- **Day 1 defaults removed.** The 6 and 2.40 Å defaults are gone from `ion.py`. `build.yaml` uses `evidence: ca_hydration`, and typing either value there raises an error.
- **Where the row_ids are recorded.** The source ids of `ca_hydrated.extxyz`, `smoke_interface.extxyz` and `hero_interface.extxyz` carry them, and so does each render settings log (`inputs[].evidence_row_ids`).

### Composition (`src/nacre/structures/hero.py`, `structures/preprint/hero_interface.extxyz`, 3878 atoms, charge 0)

- **Slab.** Hydroxylated α-Al₂O₃(0001) in a rectangular periodic cell: new `orthogonal` slab option, 9 × 5 cells (43.2 × 41.6 Å), 4 O layers.
- **Six oligomer chains.** These are the generic four-unit catechol chain from the conformer record, each anchored bidentate: its end catechol O take the places of two neighbouring surface hydroxyl O, which removes 2 surface OH and 2 phenolic H, so each anchor is neutral. Each chain is a rigid body turned about the O–O axis; the lowest-lying clash-free orientation within 35° of the requested azimuth is taken. The chains lean, with mean heights of 6–7 Å above the hydroxyl H and tips at 11–14 Å.
- **Three bound Ca²⁺** sit at catecholate sites near chain tips (catechol monoanion, bidentate, coordination number 7: 2 catecholate O and 5 waters, all at 2.37 Å). This is a few ions, not a cloud: binding adds no driving force (derivation section 7), and a test caps the number of Ca outside the nucleus.
- **The nucleus.** A first ACC nucleus drawn as a spherical cap with a **50° contact angle** on the catechol layer:
  - base radius 11 Å, base plane 6.5 Å above the hydroxyl H;
  - 12 of 12 formula units placed;
  - Ca and CO₃ inserted under the nacre.structures.acc distance rules, so it fills around the chains it wets.
- **One bridging Ca²⁺** sits at the cap rim (12.5 Å from the cap axis, 8.8 Å up), bound to a chain catecholate. A nucleus carbonate replaces the shell water that points most toward the cap, so this Ca is 7-coordinate (2 catecholate O, 4 waters, 1 carbonate O).
- **Charge balance (D5).** The four Ca sites are +1 each, balanced by **two dissolved CO₃²⁻**. The explicit ledger is in the file metadata and sums to 0.
- **Water.** 411 molecules at 1 g/cm³, only behind a cut-away plane at y = 0.62 of the cell, so the interface in front reads.
- **Physics constraint.** The nucleus–template contact is the focus. Ca²⁺ appears only as three bound ions and the bridge; there is no Ca²⁺ cloud and no free Ca²⁺.

### Choices made

- **Why the cap sits on the layer.** A search over bridge-chain placements (anchor positions on rings around the cap, azimuths, units 1 to 3) found no clash-free Ca²⁺ on a catechol low enough to sit at the alumina. Every feasible bridging Ca sat 7.4 to 9 Å up, on the first hump of the chain. The cap therefore sits on the catechol layer, which matches the brief's "wetting the layer", with the bridge at its base rim.
- **Periodic-edge hydroxyls fixed by cropping.** The frame is cropped inside the cell with the bond-aware clip box (3–40 × 2.5–38.5 Å). Tests check that every kept Al has 6 drawn O, every slab O hangs on an Al, and no H is alone.
- **Hard failures, never silent fallbacks.** Equidistant anchor sites are refused. A Ca with less than 2.6 Å clearance, or a shell water that would clash, is refused rather than drawn.
