- **4b Sobol (independent marginals).** The design is Saltelli, with the Saltelli 2010 first-order and Jansen total-order estimators on centred outputs. CIs are percentile bootstrap intervals over base rows, vectorised as count-matrix products in seeded blocks of 50, so no bootstrap array exceeds about 52 MB at 2¹⁷ rows.
  - **Sizing.** The target half-width is stated before sizing: 0.01 at 95 % in `screen.yaml`. A pilot run gives h₀, and the base size is n = pilot (h₀/target)², rounded up to a power of two.
  - **Convergence by doubling.** The 2n run contains the n run as its first half. Converged means the 2n half-width ≤ target and the largest shift of any index ≤ target.
  - **Cap.** In production, if 2n would exceed `max_base` (2¹⁷), the run is capped and records `capped: true` and `converged: false` with the achieved half-width, so a shortfall is reported, not hidden.
- **Sensitivity scale.** Positive multiplicative quantities (Ω, E_enr, c_local, barriers, r*, N_nuc) are analysed as log10. On the linear scale the barriers' 1/ln²Ω divergence as Ω → 1⁺ let a few near-saturation draws carry the variance. On the mock table at N = 1024, the pilot half-width of the barrier was 2.6×10⁴ on the linear scale and 0.23 on log10. The scale is recorded per quantity.
- **4c Dependent inputs.** `nacre.screen.shapley` computes Shapley effects (Owen 2014; Song, Nelson and Staum 2016):
  - cost c(J) = E[Var(Y | X₋J)], estimated by double-loop Monte Carlo under the copula;
  - an all-subsets design up to 14 players, random permutations with subset caching beyond;
  - effects normalised to sum to one;
  - bootstrap CIs resampling the outer draws.

  Per link, the players are the link's inputs plus every input correlated with one of them.

## Task 6: required precision

`configs/preprint/precision_method.md` (`33eb102`, made consistent with the frozen statistic in `003687d`) defines the method.

- **Definition.** The required precision of a governing parameter is the largest contraction `s*` of its prior, about the median in its stated variable (log for log-uniform priors and log10 keys), at which the verdict is decided, holding the others at their priors.
- **Frozen statistic.** Under the frozen band-mass rule at credible level p, decided means the edge lies outside the equal-tailed interval at level 2p − 1. A test checks this equivalence against the band-mass verdict.
- **Algorithm.** Common random numbers, a grid scan for monotonicity, and bisection to 1e-4 in `s`.
- **Status values.** `already_clears`, `ok` and `unreachable`; the schema has `required_precision`.
- **Benchmark against the closed form.**

  | Case | Estimated `s*` | Exact `s*` |
  |---|---|---|
  | independent | 0.8543 | 0.8537 |
  | ρ = 0.5 | 0.7812 | 0.7834 |

  The `already_clears` and `unreachable` cases are confirmed. The contraction keeps the median and scales central widths by `s` on uniform, log-uniform and log10-normal priors. P0-W4.4 applies it.
