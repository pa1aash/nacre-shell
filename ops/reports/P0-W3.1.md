## Sign convention (Young)

The contact angle θ is measured through the nucleus at the three-phase line. Young's relation is cosθ = (γ_sl − γ_sn)/γ_nl. In words, cosθ is the free energy gained per unit contact area by replacing substrate–liquid interface with substrate–nucleus interface, divided by the nucleus–liquid energy. θ = 0 means the nucleus wets the template completely (f = 0, no barrier); θ = π means no wetting (f = 1, the homogeneous barrier).

The water-displacement term is defined as Δγ_w ≡ γ_sn − γ_sn^(0). It is the free energy per unit contact area to displace the template-bound water layer under the nucleus, and **Δγ_w > 0 is a cost**. It sits on the substrate side, γ_sn = γ_sn^(0) + Δγ_w, so ∂cosθ/∂Δγ_w = −1/γ_nl < 0 and ∂f/∂Δγ_w = 3 sin²θ/(4γ_nl) ≥ 0: a cost raises θ, f and ΔG*_het. When γ_sn is supplied directly as a full in-water interfacial free energy, Δγ_w ≡ 0 and the term must not be added again. Sign-flip test SF1 encodes this, and S_spread carries the term with the same sign.

## γ_eff in Young

**No.** Young and ΔG*_hom use the bare γ_nl. The deck's γ_eff = γ_nl − Δγ_w is kept only as a named diagnostic (`gamma_eff_deck`, `cos_theta_deck`) for the double-counting demonstration (C3 item 13). It is rejected for three reasons:

1. **Geometry.** The displaced water sits on the contact disc. The cap's free surface, whose energy is γ_nl·A_cap, never touches the template.
2. **Consistency.** The spherical-cap factorisation ΔG*_het = f(θ)·ΔG*_hom holds only if the same γ_nl appears in Young and multiplies A_cap (derivation, Section 5). A modified denominator leaves a residual surface term.
3. **Sign.** With γ_eff in the denominator, ∂cosθ/∂Δγ_w = (γ_sl − γ_sn^(0))/γ_eff² > 0 on the wetting side, so a displacement cost would lower the barrier. This is design flag 3.

The checker proves both derivative signs symbolically, using the reparametrisation γ_sl = γ_sn^(0) + u and γ_nl = Δγ_w + v with u, v > 0. It also confirms them on an exact rational witness: registered f(+, 0, −) = 833/2048 > 81/256 > 475/2048; deck f(+) = 100/343 < f(0) = 81/256.

## Open questions for cp2

`theory/open_questions.md` lists 16 questions. Seven are priority A, meaning they can change the sign of a verdict:

- **Q1. Bookkeeping.** The local-ideal enrichment bookkeeping is registered, against the grand-canonical alternative. Under bookkeeping B (registered), ΔG_seq < 0 always lowers the barrier. Under bookkeeping A, Ca²⁺ adsorption that stabilises only the template–water interface raises θ. The choice is therefore not neutral, and P0-W3.2 must settle it.
- **Q2. Carbonate at bulk activity.** This is an upper estimate of Ω_local. If the Ca²⁺ enrichment is purely electrostatic, the Ca²⁺ and CO₃²⁻ Boltzmann factors are reciprocal and Ω_local = Ω_bulk: only specific binding raises the ion product.
- **Q6. Placement and sign of Δγ_w.** This is the convention above.
- **Q8. Pourbaix.** The choice of dissolved-ion reference can make the screen pass by construction, and the ACC phase is never on the hull.
- **Q9. Li-open window.** The window is unbounded on the anodic side for a Li-free phase such as CaCO₃. As constructed, it declares anodic stability by construction.
- **Q11.** One supersaturation variable (Ω, not the per-ion S); priority C.
- **Q13.** Structural water in the ion activity product of ACC; priority B.

The two priority-B and priority-C items are listed with Q1, Q2, Q6, Q8 and Q9 because they touch the same equations.
