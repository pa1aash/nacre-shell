**B3 result.** No source pairs a Ca2+-template binding free energy with γ_eff on the same surface. The
evidence splits into two cases:

- **Crystal binding to the template.** Calcite binding to SAMs (Hamm 2014, Nielsen 2020, measured by force
  spectroscopy) lowers γ_eff linearly. A phosphate SAM binds Ca2+ strongly in solution, yet it binds calcite
  most weakly and has the highest γ_eff.
- **Ca2+-binding groups on a hydrophilic film.** Adding such groups raises γ_eff or leaves it unchanged
  (Knight 2024, Giuffre 2013; contrary = y). The explanation offered is that water is harder to displace.

The evidence is recorded in `lit/conflicts.md` section 8 with row ids. It agrees with W3's contract-v2
reading (Ca2+ binding at fixed γ_sn raises the C3 barrier). It is recorded as evidence, not as a ruling.

## Task D: loader

`src/nacre/lit/params.py` provides `load_params`, `validate`, `prior`, `priors`, `prior_set`, `resolve`,
`correlation_matrix` and `sample_correlated`, plus a command, `python -m nacre.lit.params`.

The validator checks:

- units parse with pint, are coherent SI, and match the unit in `theory/symbols.yaml`;
- the range contains the central value, and every null range has a reason;
- every source row exists, and `best_grade` matches the sources;
- the prior parameters fit the README clause named in the entry;
- the correlation groups are complete, symmetric, have a unit diagonal and are positive semi-definite;
- each alias copy matches its entry.

**Compatibility with W4.** `prior()` returns a `ParamPrior`, a subclass of `nacre.screen.priors.Prior`.
`priors()` returns a tuple with `.correlation`. `prior_set()` returns `nacre.screen.priors.PriorSet`.
The screen's `sample_inputs` and `saltelli_design` take them unchanged (tested).

Two consequences for W4:

- `nacre.screen.priors.load_priors` cannot read `lit/params.yaml` directly, because the alias views carry
  non-sampled entries. W4 should import `nacre.lit.params`.
- Keys with `transform: log10` are sampled in log10 units.

## Decisions taken in this session

- **Rule path.** The rule is at `lit/corpus/params_README.md` (ownership hook). Integrator: add
  `lit/params_README.md` to p0-w1's ownership, or keep this path.
- **D1 key.** Numeric rows dedupe on doi + locator + quantity + symbol + value; a row with no value uses its
  printed range as the value. Day 1's key also included range and unit; the merge result was unchanged.
- **E3 independence.** Distinct determinations in one paper (facets, surfaces, ligands, ionic strengths)
  are separate values. They count as one independent source for the sourced or single-source status.
  The compilation rows of the calcite-water γ table name their originals.
- **Units.** Concentrations printed per litre are read per kilogram for ionic strength in dilute aqueous
  solution, as stated in the entry. Salt molality and molarity in organic solvents are kept as separate
  keys.
- **pH.** Unmodified alkaline slurries (`ph_slurry`, the charter regime) are kept apart from slurries
  with pH modifiers (`ph_slurry_modified`).
- **Proxies.** Excluded under E2 and named as comparators: alumina calorimetric enthalpies, Al oxide
  tunnel barriers, Tiron, and the sulfonated-film and mineral γ_eff values.
