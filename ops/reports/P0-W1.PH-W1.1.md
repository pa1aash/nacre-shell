## Al-salt ladder and passivation window
Window sources found: **4.5-8.5** (RSC Sustainability 2023, p. 379, quoting Passerini and co-workers, D) and **4-8.5 at 0.1 mM Al3+** (Kuhnel 2016, from the Al Pourbaix diagram, C). The KIT slides have no DOI and could not be fetched. Al in LiBOB (Myung 2010, class A): re-passivates at 3, 4, 5 V; the film is an Al-O compound by ToF-SIMS, not an identified borate (contrary); borate/oxalate at 4.5-5.3 V and AlBO3 are second-hand (D). Al(PO3)3 reacts with surface Li2CO3/LiOH to give Li3PO4+AlPO4 (abstract). AlPO4 hydrates are metastable against gibbsite above pH about 3 (Lagno, abstract).
