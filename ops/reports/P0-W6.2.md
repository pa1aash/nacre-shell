## Task B: Fig 3, the thermodynamic cycle

- **Source.** `theory/derivation_body.tex` §7, W3 commit as recorded in `figures/tikz/schematics.yaml` (`fig3.w3_commit`; see "W3 contract v3" below). The states and arrows are those of §7, with no step added:
  - **A, bulk reservoir.** Hydrated Ca²⁺ and CO₃²⁻ at the bulk chemical potentials.
  - **B, template–liquid interface.** Ca²⁺ adsorbed at catecholate and carboxylate sites, with γ_sl = γ_sl⁽⁰⁾ + Δγ_ads (Gibbs–Langmuir).
  - **C, cap on the template.** The template–nucleus contact (γ_sn), with Ca²⁺ bridging catecholate and carboxylate sites to the crystal as the contact excess Γ_Ca,sn inside γ_sn (cp2 fix F1). Also the nucleus–liquid surface (γ_nl), the template–liquid interface outside the contact (γ_sl), and Young's relation at the contact line.
  - **Arrow 1, A→B.** ΔG_seq, entering only γ_sl (blue, the same colour as the γ_sl interface).
  - **Arrow 2, B→C.** Cap formation.
  - **Arrow 3, A→C.** The nucleus is fed from the reservoir at k_BT ln Ω_bulk. No arrow runs from the bound layer to the nucleus, and only two ions are bound, so nothing reads as a raised driving force.
- **Default figure (R7).** No text, 17.1 cm × 6.0 cm. The interface colours are blue γ_sl, vermillion γ_sn and black γ_nl. The first choice for γ_nl, bluish green, merged with blue under tritanopia (ΔE 11.7); black clears ΔE 49.
- **Symbol variant (`figures/out/variants/fig3_symbols.pdf`).** The same drawing with ΔG_seq, γ_sl, γ_sn and γ_nl in Arial 7 pt, built with XeLaTeX and `fontspec`. `sansmath` lacked the upright Δ glyph, and LuaLaTeX lacks `luatex85.sty` in BasicTeX, so neither was used.
