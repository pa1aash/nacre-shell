### Double-counting demonstration: directions

The comparison is partial wetting with enrichment, ΔG_seq < 0. The wrong variants shift an interfacial energy by Γ_s ΔG_seq inside Young, on top of Ω_local.

- **Shift on γ_sn** (bound Ca²⁺ credited with stabilising the template-nucleus contact): the wrong barrier is **lower** than the contract barrier. Double counting overstates the template's benefit. With ΔG_seq > 0 (depletion) the direction reverses. This is asserted at a fixed point and at random partial-wetting points.
- **Shift on γ_sl** (a Gibbs-adsorption-like lowering of the template-liquid energy): the wrong barrier is **higher**. The sign of the double-counting error therefore depends on which interface absorbs the binding free energy. This is exactly the physical question P0-W3.2 must settle (the Gibbs adsorption link between Ca²⁺ binding and γ_sl).
- **Water cost counted twice** (Δγ_w inside the deck's γ_eff and again in γ_sn), with Δγ_w > 0 and γ_sl − γ_sn > 0: the wrong barrier is **lower**. γ_eff < γ_nl shrinks ΔG*_hom by (γ_eff/γ_nl)³ and raises cos θ.
