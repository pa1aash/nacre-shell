**Interpretation for the planning chat.** W1 widens the template-binding and Ca2+ transfer free energies only. The ion-pair and hydrolysis constants (`log10_k_cahco3`, `log10_k_caco3_aq`, `log10_k_caoh`) are single-source speciation constants from critically evaluated databases. They are flagged `single_source` but not widened, because m_seq is the error of computed Ca2+-ligand binding, not of these measurements. Widening them by about 1.26 log units would move the carbonate speciation substantially. Reverse this if the gap policy meant every single-source constant.

## R10 and R2 outcomes

**Solvation of Ca2+ and Al3+ relative to Li+ (R10): gap.**
- No open source gives a single-ion transfer Gibbs energy of Ca2+ or Al3+ from water into any registered solvent (PC, EC, DMC, EC:DMC, EC:EMC, ethyl isopropyl sulfone) or any other aprotic solvent.
- Marcus 1983 lists Ca2+ only as unselected. Al3+ is absent from Marcus 1983 and Gritzner 1988/1990. Li+ has a value only for PC (`transfer_free_energy_li_water_pc`).
- `solvation_ca_vs_li` and `solvation_al_vs_li` stay gaps with these reasons. No R-cell pass can count while they are gaps (F5).
- Flagged aqueous analogs run beside them: `solvation_ca_vs_li_water_analog` (−555 kJ mol^-1) and `solvation_al_vs_li_water_analog` (−3100 kJ mol^-1), from the Marcus hydration energies (grade D, read through a quoting source).

**Ca(ClO4)2 entropy bound (R10/R2): found, as an analog bound.**
- No table gives S of anhydrous Ca(ClO4)2 or of any anhydrous alkaline-earth perchlorate; only the tetrahydrate (TK-023).
- The bound is the anion-exchange analog S(CaX2) + 2 [S(MClO4) − S(MX)] over X = Cl, NO3 and M = Li, Na, K, with entropies from USGS B2131 (2σ) and NBS TN 270-6/270-8. It spans 229 to 265 J K^-1 mol^-1 (`s_ca_clo4_2_analog_low`, `_high`).
- With M = Li it is the exchange entropy of Ca + 2 LiX → CaX2 + 2 Li carried to the perchlorate.
- Checks:
  - the alkaline-earth entropies rise Mg < Ca < Sr < Ba within the chlorides and within the nitrates;
  - the tetrahydrate lies above the bound.
- The neglected exchange entropy is bounded: |ΔS_ex| ≤ 28.7 J K^-1 mol^-1 (`exchange_entropy_bound`).

**m_expt budget (R2): `window_margin_expt` = 0.087 V** (envelope 0.073 to 0.142 V) for CaCO3 + 2 LiClO4 → Ca(ClO4)2 + CO2 + ½ O2 + 2 Li.
- In W5's exchange route ΔG = ΔfH(Ca(ClO4)2) − 2 ΔfH(LiClO4) + ΔfG(CO2) − ΔfG(CaCO3), because the LiClO4 free energies cancel.
- The budget is the linear sum of the tabulated uncertainties plus 298.15 K times the entropy bound, over 2F:
