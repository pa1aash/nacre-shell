## Physics constraint, as built

- Binding raises no driving force; the template acts only through how well the nucleus wets it.

### Fixes applied (all three)

1. **Lens nucleus.**
   - Shape: a spherical cap with a flat base, contact angle 50°, base radius 15 Å (sphere radius 19.6 Å, height 7.0 Å, volume 2651 Å³).
   - The base plane is 11 Å above the hydroxyl H, resting on the catechol layer, whose chain tops are at 12–13 Å.
   - Filling: Ca²⁺ and CO₃²⁻ inserted alternately under the `nacre.structures.acc` minimum-distance rules; 33 of 33 formula units placed.
   - Tested: no Ca or C below the base plane, and at least 8 ions within 2 Å of it.
2. **One representation.**
   - Ball-and-stick for every element and species in every scene, at atom radius 0.28 × van der Waals (ASE table) and sticks 0.14 Å. Space-filling is gone.
   - The template's sticks are 0.19 Å; atom radii stay uniform.
   - The nucleus is the focus through position, its own rim light and (in B) depth of field.
3. **Water.**
   - The structure has water throughout the cell (3248 molecules at 1 g/cm³).
   - The renderer draws only a thin shell behind the plane through the lens axis. Opacity is 0.2 within 2.5 Å of the slab, template, ions or nucleus, falling linearly to zero at 7 Å; fainter molecules are not drawn. Opacity comes from a per-vertex attribute, so molecules fade individually.
4. **Template.**
   - Five chains spread across the width. Three run under the lens, in rows from alternating sides; two sit in the side strips.
   - Every chain is anchored bidentate through its end catechol O to Al (tested: two O within 2.15 Å of Al per chain).
   - The template uses a longer generic chain (6 units, 24 Å, recorded conformer, MMFF94), so three rows carpet the lens base.
   - Crops keep whole molecules (a new `clip: {molecules: true}`: a bonded group is drawn whole when its centroid is in the box, otherwise not at all). The camera frames every drawn atom, so nothing is cut at the frame edge.
5. **Ions.**
   - Three hydrated Ca²⁺ at catecholate sites: one on the left strip chain, two on the right strip chain.
   - One bridging Ca²⁺ under the left lens rim. One nucleus carbonate completes its shell, so it touches both the template and the nucleus.
   - Two dissolved carbonates out in solution, 17 Å up and well clear of the nucleus.
6. **Slab.** Three O layers (two Al pairs) in an 85.7 × 41.2 Å orthogonal cell, cropped inside the cell by the bond-aware crop. Every kept Al has 6 O and no hydroxyl floats free (tested for each of the three scenes).
7. **Lighting.** All of it in `rig.py`, documented in its docstring:
   - the day 2 key, fill and rim rig;
   - a subtle nucleus rim light (azimuth 165°, irradiance 3.0 against the key's 4.0), light-linked so that it lights the nucleus only;
   - a Cycles shadow catcher under the slab for a soft ground shadow;
   - ambient occlusion as before;
   - atmospheric depth fading (haze towards a pale grey beyond 35 % of the depth range, up to a 0.45 mix at the far end);
   - a transparent film.

These are the committed renders (second full pass, 06:20–07:09). Earlier passes are described below.

- **Memory gate.** No render waited: every reading was at or above 34 % free.
- **The 2 MB cap.**
  - The first passes at about 1600 px came out at 2.10 MB (A, 1600 × 900), 2.93 MB (B, 1600 × 900) and 2.50 MB (C, 1600 × 1200). Roughly half their pixels are partially transparent (the depth-of-field blur, the shadow catcher and the faded water), and those compress poorly.
  - Lossless re-encoding saved under 3 %.
  - The canvases were therefore reduced (A 1520, B 1280, C 1400 px wide, same framing), so the explicit 2 MB cap (taken as 2,000,000 bytes) takes precedence over "about 1600 px". First-pass timings were 415 s (A), 466 s (B) and 670 s (C) wall, with 45–51 % free at start.
- **Anchor and Ca bonds.**
  - A test of the crops found that bonds were drawn only within a scene component. The first passes therefore lacked the catechol O–Al anchor bonds, the bound Ca²⁺–catecholate bonds and the bridging Ca²⁺–nucleus bond.
  - `config.cross_component_bonds` now adds Al–O and Ca–O bonds between opaque atoms of different components (hero A: 12 anchor, 6 bound, 2 bridge–chain and 1 bridge–nucleus half-bond pairs). Every render log records the count.
  - All eight renders were redone after this fix, with the A and B section plane moved from 14 to 12.5 Å so that the bridging carbonate stays in the cut.
- **Previews.** Scratch previews at 320–640 px and 8 samples were rendered while composing (all with 29 % or more free); they are not kept. All passes together: first pass 04:22–05:16, B/C/A canvas re-renders 05:21–05:58, final pass 06:20–07:09.

  - Water insertion skips atoms beyond the cutoff in z before the minimum-image distances; the committed day 2 water files regenerate byte-identically.

  - The scene runner refuses any object other than meshes, the camera and lights, and counts text-type objects into the log (0 in every render).
