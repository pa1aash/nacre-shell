## C3 and C4 under the rule

**C3.** On the admitted draws, γ_sl is non-negative but small: median 1.2 mJ m⁻² (5 % to 95 %: 0.13 to 2.9) at the detail point. A favourable template needs γ_sn <= γ_sl - m_cos γ_nl (thresholds C3, D27/A5): the crystal-template contact must be at least as favourable as the template-water contact. Under the γ_sn ignorance prior U[0, γ_p + γ_nv] that wedge carries P(γ_sn <= γ_sl) = 0.0019 over the admitted draws, so C3a has P(f <= 1/2) = 0.003 there: **falsified on the admitted draws at every design point and framing**. The reason is now physical rather than an artefact: binding lowers γ_sl toward zero (the admissible set allows it down to zero, not below), and a template whose water contact is already nearly free of cost cannot be preferred by the nucleus unless γ_sn is nearly zero. The clipped boundary θ = π on an admitted draw needs γ_sn >= γ_sl + γ_nl; it no longer comes from a negative γ_sl.

- Where 1 - M_adm < p_cred (Ca_T <= 1e-3): C3 falsified (restricted), labelled conditional on admissibility and on the analog.
- Where 1 - M_adm >= p_cred (Ca_T >= 1e-2): **inconclusive (inadmissible inputs)**, the restricted verdict (falsified) printed beside. This is a statement about the inputs: the analog binding constant combined with the sourced γ_sl0 and Γ_s places at least the credible mass on states where the interface does not exist.
- Headline C3: design-dependent, inconclusive (24 points falsified, 12 inadmissible). C3b stays a gap (A_patch).

**C4.** Inconclusive at every point, as in P0-W4.3: the spreading route fails (P(S_spread >= m_S) = 0; P(γ_sl > γ_nl) = 0 at both states on the admitted draws), the closure route is a gap (d_max), and a falsification needs both routes.
