## Decisions

- **Thread-scoped retrieval wrapper instead of `nacre retrieve`.** `nacre retrieve` appends to
  `lit/instrument_gaps.csv`, which is outside this lane's ownership, and five parallel writers would race
  on one ledger, one record store and one cache. The wrapper in `src/nacre/lit/thread.py` reuses
  `nacre.retrieval` (HTTP, rate limits, clients, store) and redirects its paths to the thread's directory
  and to `.local/`. `src/nacre/retrieval/**` was not edited.
- **Added routes.**
  - OpenAlex for search, abstracts and citation chaining, because Semantic Scholar returns HTTP 429
    without a key.
  - Europe PMC and PubMed, plus abstracts from Crossref, OpenAlex, Europe PMC and Semantic Scholar.
  - Full text from all open-access locations reported by Unpaywall and OpenAlex, then Europe PMC
    XML. When a publisher's bot challenge blocks a direct download (HTTP 403), the archived snapshot of
    that same open-access location is used. No paywall was circumvented: the snapshot fallback applies
    only to locations listed as open access.
- **Unpaywall contact.** palaashgang@gmail.com, from `configs/retrieval/contact.yaml`.
- **Merge key.** A literal deduplication on DOI, locator and quantity dropped 47 rows, 43 of which were
  distinct values printed in one table (for example pKa1 and pKa2, or four hydrolysis constants). The key
  was refined:
  - Numeric rows dedupe on DOI, locator, quantity, symbol, value, range and unit.
  - Qualitative rows dedupe on DOI, locator and quantity, across threads only.

  The result is 3 true duplicates. This reads the session's dedupe rule as "the same datum recorded
  twice"; the integrator may rule otherwise.
- **Split targets.**
  - Section 3d's "nucleus-water interfacial energy (ACC, calcite)" became two targets, one per framing,
    because the framing is open until G2.
  - Substrate-water γ became two targets, oxide and organic.
  - Ksp, molar volume and the two Ca2+ binding targets were split per polymorph or ligand. That gives 23
    targets in total.
- **Coverage independence.** The mechanical status stays as the session defines it. An orchestrator file,
  `coverage_notes.yaml`, records caveats and counts of independent originals, so that W1.2 does not read
  "covered" as "constrained".
- **Scope.**
  - The validator applies the scope-guard patterns to every row field.
  - A test applies them to every committed file under `lit/`.
  - Threads recorded only equilibrium, structural and potential quantities; dissolution kinetics and
    currents were excluded.
  - One logged exact-title query in `lit/corpus/T-A/queries.jsonl` reproduced a third-party title that
    matches pattern SG04. Its `query` string was replaced by "exact-title search for
    10.1016/j.ensm.2019.08.020", with a `redacted` field saying so; route, date, hits and DOIs are unchanged.
- **Grading.**
  - Abstract-only rows are capped at D, and compilations at C, both enforced.
  - Values back-calculated through CNT are C, as the README defines them.
- **arXiv identifiers.** Rows cite the DataCite DOIs `10.48550/arxiv.<id>`. Their metadata came from the
  arXiv client, since Crossref returns 404 for them.
