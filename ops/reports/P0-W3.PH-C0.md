## Protocol rules (theory/protocol_computational.md; machine form screen_ph.links.K1.premise.computed)
- Target: Delta_G_tr_rel = DG_tr(M^z+) - z DG_tr(Li+), water to PC, one method. The per-charge scale offset of the data and of the method cancels. E_PC = E_aq + Delta_G_tr_rel/(n F), so >= 0 makes E_aq = E_lb a lower bound. H+ (r3) is not computed.
- Method: CREST/GFN2-xTB gas-phase geometries and thermal terms (same in every solvent); PBE0-D3(BJ)/def2-TZVP single points; SMD if the code has it for every solvent, else IEF-PCM for all. PySCF or Psi4 picked at PH-C1 by a feature check that computes no energy. Cluster cycle written out for water and PC. Cluster n (same in every solvent): Li+ 4; Na+ to Cs+, Ca2+, Al3+, Cu2+, Zn2+, Cd2+, Hg2+ 6; La3+ 8; n_max 8; n-1 sensitivity for Li+, Ca2+, Al3+. Cap 4 h per job on 16 cores (capped means not computed, which means unresolved). No tuning after the first benchmark job.
- Benchmark first, and committed and hashed before any Ca2+/Al3+ job. Acceptance set A: Na+, K+, Rb+, Cs+ against Li+ in PC, TATB selected (TI-077, TT-092 to TT-095; N = 4). TT-096 to TT-105 give the data spread. **R_cap 12 kJ/mol (the plan said 15, tightened).** Reason: (ii) f_2 R_cap/(2F) must not exceed the smallest open Ca-row margin (Ca(PO3)2 glass m_expt 0.1348 V, margins_v6). At 15 it is 0.155 V (fails); at 12 it is 0.124 V. On (i), the two TATB determinations agree on the relative quantity to within about 3 kJ/mol (data inspection only), so 12 is well above the data's own spread. Failure gives no computed pass anywhere: `unresolved (benchmark failed)`.
- Cu2+/Hg2+ (TT-106, TT-107) and the other-solvent multivalent rows (TT-108 to TT-117) check only the charge dependence (Jahn-Teller and soft ions) and never enter R_bench. A row counts only if its relative quantity can be formed on one scale (a same-scale monovalent reference in the same solvent). A counted row passes if abs(calc - meas) <= f_z R_bench, and a failure makes that charge unresolved. Rows that cannot be anchored are reported as `scale-unanchored`. La3+ MeCN (TT-116) is a bound check.
- Hard multivalent benchmark: La3+/MeOH (TT-117) if a TATB monovalent key in MeOH exists; or Ca2+/MeCN TT-118 (the Rb+ = 0 scale forms Ca2+ - 2 Rb+ by construction; proposed registration). Without one, Ca2+ and Al3+ are labelled `extrapolated (ion type)` and reach at most indicative.
- Uncertainty: u_tr = f_z R_bench, with f 2 (Ca2+) and 3 (Al3+), or 2 for both if a hard multivalent benchmark exists. An ion is resolved only if u_tr < abs(D).
- Bracket rule (claims_redirect 2f, 10e; thresholds Section 6): the premise is measured, OR computed with D - k_q u_tr >= 0 at one-sided q = 95 %. k_q is the Student-t quantile with N_bench = 4 dof (2.13; the normal would give 1.645). The pass edge is E_lb >= E_c + m_1 + u_tr/(Delta_n_Li F) (linear sum), labelled `pass (computed premise)` everywhere.
