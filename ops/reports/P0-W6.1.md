## 2d Figure system (`src/nacre/figs`, `nacre figs`)

- **`cli.py`.** Auto-discovered `register(subparsers)`: `nacre figs [--mock] [--only figN …] [--tiff] [--no-cvd]`.
- **`build.py`.** Discovers `figures/src/figN.py`, validates the input against its schema and enforces the mock boundary:
  - mock input must carry `mock: true` and writes only to `figures/out/mock/`;
  - real input must not carry it and never writes there.

  It renders at the exact RSC column width, runs the checker before saving, and writes PDF (Type 42) and a 600 dpi PNG with date metadata removed. Rebuilds are byte-identical. It also writes the CVD simulations and `manifest.json`, which holds the claim, caption key and sha256 of the input and every output.
- **`panels.py`.** `panel_letters(axes)` sets bold lower-case letters as the axes' left title, above the plot area, tagged `gid=panel-letter`.
- **`checker.py`.** An allow-list walk of every artist.
  - **Rejected:** legends (axes and figure), any non-title text in axes, annotations, arrow patches, offset boxes or callouts, titles other than tagged panel letters, figure-level text, suptitles or artists, and unknown artist types.
  - **Also enforced:**
    - panel letters and colour bars must not overlap a plot area;
    - text is at least 7 pt and in one font family;
    - every visible stroke is at least 0.5 pt (zero-width means no stroke);
    - the width is exactly one RSC column and the height is at most 23.3 cm.
- **Support modules:**
  - `schema.py`: a small validator for the JSON Schema subset the schemas use;
  - `cvd.py`: PNG simulations;
  - `colours.py`: CVD-checked fill sets;
  - `axes.py`: plain 1-2-5 log ticks;
  - `symbols.py`: axis titles.

| Figure | Size | Claim it will carry |
|---|---|---|
| fig4, retrodiction outcome | single, 7.0 cm | Cases with an observed continuous carbonate layer have predicted heterogeneous barriers below the critical barrier; cases without a layer lie above it. |
| fig5, nucleation-barrier map | single, 7.0 cm | The heterogeneous barrier falls below the critical barrier only at small wetting angle and high supersaturation; the threshold contours bound the region where a layer can nucleate. |
| fig6, Sobol ranking and precision | double, 6.5 cm | A few inputs carry most of the variance of the predicted barrier, and those inputs need the tightest precision from first-principles validation. |
| fig7, Pourbaix for both regimes | double, 7.0 cm | The window of pH and potential in which the protective solid phase is stable differs between the two regimes. |

How each template encodes its data:

- **fig4.** Per case, the predicted barrier is drawn with its range as a horizontal bar on a log axis. The observed outcome is encoded twice, as colour and as marker shape and fill. A vertical line marks the critical barrier.
- **fig5.** The barrier is shown on a log colour scale (`batlow`) with a colour bar outside the axes. Thresholds are black contour lines with no inline labels.
- **fig6.** (a) S₁ (filled circles) and S_T (open diamonds) with confidence intervals, ranked by S_T. (b) Required precision on a log axis, in the same order.
- **fig7.** Domain fills come from `colours.distinct_fills`: blue, vermillion, sky blue and yellow at 5 % lightening, with a minimum ΔE of 21.1 under all conditions. The first choice, cycle order at 45 % lightening, collapsed under tritanopia and was replaced. Dashed lines mark the water limits.

Every key and identification is in each template's `caption_key`, for the caption.
