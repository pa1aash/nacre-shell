## Day 2 science summary

- **C2 role.** C2 is a reported binding descriptor. It reaches C3 only through the Gibbs term Delta_gamma_ads and is not a kill criterion; the study gates on C1, C3, C4 and C5; the deletion test gates on C3 and C4.
- **R-cell onsets and the scheme question.** For CaCO3 with LiClO4 the onsets are 4.32 V (JANAF), 3.71 V (MP) and 3.94 V (all-experimental); both schemes give 3.94 V after correction by their own benchmark errors, against a 4.3 V charge cut-off. The values were seen before the scheme rule was proposed (decision 3). No R-cell pass can count while the Ca2+ and Al3+ solvation keys are gaps.
- **R-slurry falsifiability failure.** The Pourbaix screen calls calcite stable where measured solubility says it dissolves: MP ion energies put calcite about 2.5 log units below the measured Ksp and the hull tolerance adds about 4.2 (decision 2).
- **C4 branch masses.** Complete wetting needs gamma_sl > gamma_nl and non-wetting needs W_pw + W_nw > 2 gamma_w, so C4 can fail for every admissible gamma_sn whatever the ignorance prior (decision 6).
- **Hydroxyl gap.** Retrodiction of the hydroxyl termination cannot be done on current texts; accepted as a stated gap (D16).
