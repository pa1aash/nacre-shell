3. The plausible range of a hydrolysis row is taken as its prior support. The row conditions (pH, Ca2+, T) exist only in note text, so every state is H1 until they are registered.
4. A route product carries a slurry row and a cell row under one film_id (used as formed; drying is not a conversion step).

7. The prose names `f_PP_free` and `gamma_sx_ads`; the contract names (`a_PP_free`, `gamma_al_l_ads`, `gamma_al_n_ads`) are used.
