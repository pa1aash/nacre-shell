## Q1 and Q2: the result in one paragraph of physics

The template sits in an open interfacial region that exchanges ions with the slurry. At
equilibrium every ion carries the same electrochemical potential at a binding site, in the
diffuse layer and in the bulk. A nucleus at the template is therefore driven by k_B T ln
Omega_bulk.

- **Specific binding** raises the local Ca2+ concentration by exactly the amount it lowers the
  local standard potential: k_B T ln E_enr = -Delta_G_seq/N_A. The two cancel in the driving force.
- **A mean electrostatic potential** enriches Ca2+ and depletes CO3^2- by reciprocal factors.
  Those cancel in the ion product and in the neutral solid, which settles Q2.

The "local-ideal" bookkeeping of contract v1 gave the enriched ions the bulk standard state. In
doing so it assumed the layer is a reservoir held above the bulk chemical potential, and it
credited the binding free energy a second time, as driving force.

In the exact grand potential of a cap, every template-ion interaction is an excess quantity in
the interfacial energies. The Gibbs adsorption equation with the Langmuir isotherm puts the bound
Ca2+ into the template-liquid energy, and only there:

Delta_gamma_ads = -R T (Gamma_s ln(1 + x) - delta_s c_bulk), with x = E_enr c_bulk / c_max.

Under the nucleus the sites hold lattice ions at the bulk chemical potential, so gamma_sn does not
depend on a_Ca. The consequence reverses the v1 sign. At fixed gamma_sl0 and gamma_sn, stronger
binding lowers cos(theta) and raises the barrier, as an adsorbed inhibitor would. Ca2+ binding
favours nucleation only if the template-nucleus energy falls by more than the template-liquid
energy does.

A genuine local excess of driving force needs a non-equilibrium state: concentration during
drying faster than exchange, slow exchange with the reservoir, or local pH excursions. These lie
outside a thermodynamic screen.

## Screen rule (derivation, Section 7, rule)

1. The driving force is Delta_mu = k_B T ln Omega_bulk at the regime activities. E_enr, c_local
   and Omega_local_ideal never enter a barrier.
2. Delta_G_seq enters the barrier only through Delta_gamma_ads, added once to the Ca-free
   gamma_sl0. When gamma_sl is computed at the regime activities, nothing is added; this is the
   same rule as for Delta_gamma_w.
3. gamma_sn is the template-crystal contact energy with lattice ions at the sites. It carries no
   term in Delta_G_seq or a_Ca.
4. Carbonate needs no correction.
5. R-slurry stability is decided at the bulk state. An "enriched" evaluation is a labelled
   non-equilibrium sensitivity.

**B2, Q9.** Derivation Section 9, "The oxidation sink is fixed by the electrolyte", Eq. (40).
The released Ca2+ or Al3+ pairs with the salt anion A- (electroneutrality), and the electrons
plate Li from LiA:
- CaCO3 + 2 LiA -> CaA2 + CO2 + 1/2 O2 + 2 Li;
- Al2O3 + 6 LiA -> 2 AlA3 + 3/2 O2 + 6 Li.

The rule for choosing among salts has five parts:
1. only the anions of the registered salt or salts are admissible;
2. the limit is the minimum over the admissible sinks and their products;
3. a solid-salt limit is an upper bound on the onset;
4. a missing sink makes the limit "undetermined", which never passes;
5. the worst registered gas-reference scheme is used.

W5's reported limits are inputs to the decision and are not adjudicated here:
- CaCO3 about 4.27 V vs Li+/Li with the JANAF CO2 reference and 3.66 V with the Materials Project
  CO2 energy (nitrate sink);
- Al2O3 undetermined under nitrate (Al(NO3)3 absent from the database), finite under sulfate or
  chloride.

Under the thresholds rule, the CaCO3 limit is the lower of the two, and Al2O3 under nitrate stays
undetermined.

## Thresholds summary (`theory/thresholds.md`, ready at `3c9568b`)

**Shared rule.**
- The statistic is the band mass.
- feasible if `P_good >= p`, falsified if `P_bad >= p`, otherwise inconclusive, with p the
  credible level (> 1/2).
- Edges are the physical threshold moved by a systematic margin in the conservative direction.
- N2 clipping applies, with the boundary mass reported.
- A link takes its worst state; C3 accessibility uses a path sum.
- Omega_bulk only.

**Per link.**
- **C1:** W_adh_wet > m_W. The terminations enter as a population-weighted mixture.
- **C2:** Delta_G_seq < -m_seq, judged as the binding link only. Its consequence for nucleation is
  judged in C3 through Delta_gamma_ads. Theta and Gamma_Ca are reported.
- **C3:**
  - (a) f <= f_e at the single physical edge f = 1/2 (cos(theta) = m_cos, with zero proposed).
    This replaces the deck bands; the upper edge has no physical meaning and the intermediate band
    is replaced by the credible-mass rule.
  - (b) N_nuc >= N_req, i.e. DG_het <= B_acc = k_B T ln(J_0 t_p / N_req) at Omega_bulk.
  - C3 = (a) and (b).
- **C4:** chosen criterion S_spread >= m_S. Supporting condition: N_nuc >= tan^2(theta/2)/(2
  d_max^2). A falsified verdict needs both to fail.
- **C5:**
  - R-slurry: Delta_G_pbx <= n_at e_f / n_M at the bulk state, worst (pH, E) point. For the ACC
    framing the edge is (n_at e_f + Delta_ACC)/n_M. The falsifiability control must fail where it
    must.
  - R-cell: E_red + m_E <= E_cell_min and E_cell_max + m_E <= E_ox, worst reference scheme;
    undetermined never passes; a solid-salt pass is conditional on cp2 decision 5.

**Study.**
- falsified if any of C1 to C5 is falsified;
- feasible only if all five are feasible and the F1 deletion test holds (bare_oxide and PVDF each
  degrade at least one of C2, C3 or C4, by an ordinal step or a falsified-mass rise of at least
  p_deg; C1 alone does not count);
