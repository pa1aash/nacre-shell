`nacre track check <session>` passes iff all of these hold:

- the session is listed;
- its day is the open day, or the session was carried into it;
- the branch is the lane branch;
- the tree is clean;
- HEAD contains the commit on `origin/preprint/p0` that opened the current day (subject `ops(p0): open day <n>`; for day 0, the commit that added the ledger);
- every `requires` checkpoint has tag `p0-cpN`, or `g0` for cp1.

`open-day` is refused in these cases:

- the current branch is not `preprint/p0`;
- the tree is dirty;
- the day is unknown, or is not pending;
- day n-1 is not the open day;
- a carried session is not from day n-1;
- a day n-1 session has a status outside DONE, HOLD-P, GATE-C and GATE-H.

A session counts as DONE when its lane's `last_session` is a later session of that lane. Carried sessions are recorded in the new day's `carried` field.
