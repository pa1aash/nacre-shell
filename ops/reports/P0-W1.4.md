## Task B: Outlook evidence (T-O, 34 rows from 14 sources)

What the sources support:

- **Phosphate.**
  - Computed 0 K windows put the oxidation limit of crystalline Li3PO4 at about 4.2 V vs Li+/Li (TO-001, TO-007, TO-034), inside the 4 to 5 V cathode range.
  - The computed route removes O 2p electrons, with O2 release and condensation to pyro- or metaphosphate (TO-011, TO-012, TO-022).
  - Condensed phosphates (LiPO3) reach about 5 V (TO-003).
  - LiPON is measured stable to 5.0 to 5.5 V, but its computed onset is about 1.1 V, so the measured window is kinetic (TO-015, TO-020, TO-021).
- **Carbonate against phosphate.** One source groups Li2CO3, Li3PO4 and Li2SO4 together as oxidising above 4 V (TO-004). Its numeric Li2CO3 and Li2SO4 limits are in an SI table that could not be retrieved.
- **Oxalate.** Oxalate is a sacrificial oxidant, oxidised to CO2 at about 4.6 to 4.7 V (TO-026, TO-027).
- **Silicate.** Li4SiO4 is computed to oxidise at 3.41 V and LiAlSiO4 at 4.09 V (TO-002, TO-008).
- **Sulfate.** Only qualitative: oxidation above 4 V (TO-004), and a Li2SO4 layer persists after cycling with the potentials not printed (TO-030).
- **Phosphate on aluminium and phosphate additives.**
  - A secondary report of AlPO4 on Al without an anodic dissolution peak (TO-031).
  - A phosphate solvent decomposes at Al to fluorophosphate and AlF3 (TO-032).
  - The silyl phosphate TMSPa is not oxidised on a Li-rich NCM up to 4.7 V and acts chemically as an HF scavenger (TO-023, TO-024).

What they do not support:

- **Calcium salts:** no value for any calcium phosphate, hydroxyapatite, calcium oxalate, calcium sulfate or calcium silicate. The only hydroxyapatite row is a separator-cell proxy (TO-033).
- **Phosphate as a more stable anion:** no support for calling orthophosphate thermodynamically stable above about 4.2 V, or more anodically stable than carbonate.
- **Missing measurements:** no primary measurement of a phosphate layer on Al with a stated potential and electrolyte, and no oxidation potential of a phosphate additive.

**Draft Outlook sentence (for W8; numbers through macros):**
> Computed stability windows place the anodic limit of crystalline lithium orthophosphate near the same 4 V region as lithium carbonate, oxalate is oxidised sacrificially, and no anodic limit of a calcium phosphate, oxalate, sulfate or silicate has been reported, so the oxidation question of this study would have to be asked again for any other biomineral.

Rows marked contrary (the anion oxidises inside 4 to 5 V): TO-001, TO-002, TO-007, TO-015, TO-022, TO-026, TO-027, TO-034.
