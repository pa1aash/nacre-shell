### Design decision: derived head

The template has no class or style file; the page setup, fonts, header and footer and front-matter layout live inline in its example article. Its licence does not explicitly permit redistribution (S01), so none of that text is committed. `paper/main.tex` and `si.tex` contain only their own text: placeholder macros (`\nacreTitle`, `\nacreAuthors`, `\nacreAbstract`, `\nacreFootnotes`, `\nacreTitleMark`) and a single `\input{build/rsc_head}`. The head is produced from the local template at provisioning time: the template's own lines are kept byte for byte except where the sample title, author line, abstract, address footnotes, bibliography preamble and sample body are replaced. The renderer fails loudly if the template's layout no longer matches its expectations. `latexmkrc` points `TEXINPUTS`, `BSTINPUTS` and `BIBINPUTS` at the template directory and the paper directory.
