## Wording decisions

- **Sequestration.** "Ca2+ binding" is the term in the Model; "sequestration" survives only in N1. The Model says binding raises the local concentration but not the driving force, and that DeltaG_seq < 0 means enrichment at the sites (net binding is Gamma_Ca > 0; Delta_gamma_ads < 0 whenever Gamma_Ca > 0). The enrichment factor is not printed.
- **Hydrophilic.** Defined relative to the mineral (gamma_sl <= gamma_nl: water wets the template at least as favourably as it wets the mineral); a water contact angle in air does not decide it, so the screen reports the prior mass with gamma_sl > gamma_nl.
- **Disclosures.** (1) N5: the earlier rough calcite estimate, said to lie near the shape-factor edge registered later, without printing the value. N5's "the primary framing is chosen on literature grounds" is written as "the framings were chosen on literature grounds", because since cp3 R6 both framings are registered and neither is primary. (2) cp3 R2: the cell scheme values for CaCO3 with LiClO4 seen before the scheme rule. (3) cp3 R1: the Pourbaix control and the Omega values seen before the dissolution rule (`theory/thresholds.md` discloses it, so it is included). (4) The thresholds were set at cp2 and amended at cp3, each before any screen output, and frozen at cp3; "not set before every calculation the project made". "Engineered out" is not used.
- **Retrodiction.** Written to criterion.md (W2.2) and rulings D17/R9. The text says the controls and the interface mapping were added in a further commit before the run, and that the one case first committed through a local enrichment (RC11, still `Omega_local = E_enr Omega_bulk` in `cases.yaml`) is remapped. That remap is W2.3's to make.
- **Numbers in exact forms.** The allowlist gains NA09 to NA13: comparisons with 0 and 1, the Dupre water-competition coefficient, ln(1/q_0) and 1 - p_cred, ln(1 + x), theta/2 and pi/2. None is a reported value.
- **Length.** Model 2101 and Methods 2395 words, above the Day 1 brief targets (1000 to 1300; 600 to 800), because the P0-W8.3 order adds the regimes, coverage, ignorance-prior, framing, retrodiction, attribution and disclosure items. Nothing was cut (R8); the planning chat may set new targets for cp5 editing.
