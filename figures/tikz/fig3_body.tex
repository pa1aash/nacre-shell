% States and arrows taken from Section 7 (no step added):
%   A  bulk reservoir: hydrated Ca2+ and CO3 2- at the regime activities (bulk chemical potentials)
%   B  template-liquid interface at the regime Ca2+ activity: Ca2+ adsorbed at catecholate and
%      carboxylate sites; its free energy is gamma_sl = gamma_sl0 + Delta_gamma_ads (Gibbs-Langmuir)
%   C  cap on the template (grand potential of a cap; base 12.1..15.1 cm): template-nucleus
%      contact (gamma_sn, a grand-canonical excess) with Ca2+ bridging catecholate and carboxylate
%      sites to the crystal (the contact excess Gamma_Ca,sn, inside gamma_sn), nucleus-liquid
%      surface (gamma_nl), template-liquid interface outside the contact (gamma_sl); Young at the
%      contact line
%   1  A -> B  Ca2+ from the hydrated bulk to a site, Delta_G_seq; enters only gamma_sl (blue)
%   2  B -> C  the cap replaces template-liquid interface by template-nucleus contact over its base
%   3  A -> C  the formula units of the nucleus come from the reservoir at the bulk chemical
%              potentials (driving force k_B T ln Omega_bulk); none comes from the bound layer
