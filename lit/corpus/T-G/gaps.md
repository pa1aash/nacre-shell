# T-G gaps

- **ksp_acc, original Brecevic and Nielsen 1989 (10.1016/0022-0248(89)90168-1) and Clarkson et al. 1992 (10.1039/ft9928800243).** Both full-text requests ended as paywalled with no open copy. Their values reach the table only through compilations (Day 1 TC-037, TC-038, TC-041, TC-042), and the citation inside Purgstaller et al. 2019 (TG-006/TG-007 source) was not entered again. Purgstaller (TG-006) is an independent direct determination that agrees with Brecevic and Nielsen.
- **ksp_acc, Gebauer et al. 2010 proto-calcite / proto-vaterite ACC (10.1002/anie.201003220 and the German edition 10.1002/ange.201003220).** Both were paywalled. The abstract has no numbers. Its solubility data are covered in substance by Gebauer 2008 (TG-001/TG-002, open repository scan) and Kellermeier 2014 (TG-003 to TG-005), which come from the same group.
- **ksp_acc, Blue et al. 2017 GCA (10.1016/j.gca.2016.09.004) and Blue et al. 2015 GCA (10.1016/j.gca.2014.08.003).** Neither had a retrievable copy or an abstract. A crossref search for the title found only these records and a GSA 2017 meeting abstract (10.1130/abs/2017am-304317), which also had no retrievable text or abstract.
- **ksp_acc, Mergelsberg et al. 2020 GCA (10.1016/j.gca.2020.06.030), the most direct recent solubility study.** The publisher route had no open copy. The repository copy found by web search (DOE OSTI) timed out at connect, and the same host also timed out for a second work. Only the qualitative abstract row TG-010 could be entered.
- **ksp_acc temperature and ionic-strength dependence beyond 25 C.** The only primary T-dependent equation is Clarkson 1992 (paywalled; Day 1 TC-042 abstract). Kellermeier 2016 ChemPhysChem (entropy of ion association, 10.1002/cphc.201600653) was paywalled.
- **acc_excess_free_energy, calorimetric or other direct free energy.** Searches covered: crossref for Wolf/Leitner ACC calorimetry, Radha energetics of amorphous Ca1-xMgxCO3, ACC enthalpy of formation, and ACC Gibbs energy relative to calcite. Radha et al. 2012 GCA (10.1016/j.gca.2012.04.056) was paywalled, and its repository copy timed out. Wolf et al. 2001 J Therm Anal Calorim (10.1023/a:1011991124181) was paywalled with no abstract. A 2019 Chem Mater MD study (10.1021/acs.chemmater.9b02346) was paywalled, and its abstract has no numbers. No free energy measured independently of solubility was found. The rows TG-011 and TG-012 are grade-C derivations from primary solubilities. The only new calorimetric datum is an enthalpy (TG-009, anhydrous Mg-bearing ACC).
- **molar_volume_acc / density, Bolze 2002 (10.1021/la025918d), Faatz 2004 (10.1002/adma.200306030), Michel 2008 (10.1021/cm800403u), Goodwin 2010 (10.1002/adma.201000562) and Mergelsberg 2024 (10.1021/acs.jpcc.3c08311).** Bolze and Mergelsberg 2024 were paywalled. Faatz, Michel and Goodwin had no retrievable copy. Mergelsberg 2024 gives only an abstract density (TG-017), and its water content is in the paywalled text. The pycnometric densities with water contents (TG-013/TG-014) come from an open accepted manuscript. No density of ACC in situ (wet) with a known water content was found, apart from the Day 1 SAXS value of 1.62 g/cm3.
- **gamma_acc_water, second independent estimates.** Hu et al. 2012 Faraday Discuss (10.1039/c2fd20124k) was read in full: it has calcite effective energies only and no ACC-water value. Malini et al. 2017 (water-ACC interface MD, 10.1021/acs.cgd.7b00874), Smeets et al. 2017 PNAS and Cavanaugh et al. 2019 were read in full and give no ACC-water interfacial energy. Wallace et al. 2013 Science (10.1126/science.1230915) was paywalled; a university repository copy was located by web search but not fetched, and that paper concerns dense-liquid formation. Bewernitz et al. 2012 (10.1039/c2fd20080e) and Raiteri and Gale 2010 JACS (10.1021/ja108508k) were paywalled. Kawano et al. 2009 (10.1088/0953-8984/21/42/425102) had no retrievable copy. Fernandez-Martinez et al. 2012 EST (10.1021/es3014826) was paywalled, and its abstract gives a heterogeneous effective value for CaCO3 on quartz, not ACC-water. The only new estimate is an abstract value of 0.33 J/m2 (TG-018, grade D, paywalled; 10.1021/acs.chemmater.5b00145). It conflicts with the Day 1 range.
- **resistivity_caco3 (electronic resistivity or conductivity of calcite or CaCO3 films).** Searches covered: openalex and crossref for calcite single-crystal electrical conductivity, CaCO3 resistivity, CaCO3 impedance and dc conductivity, and CaCO3 dielectric and conductivity; a web search on Wada et al. 2012 was also run. Wada et al. 2012 Phys Chem Minerals (10.1007/s00269-012-0530-z) was paywalled with no abstract. Compton 1993 and Gabrielli 1997 (impedance of CaCO3 scales, 10.1007/bf00241910 and 10.1016/s0013-4686(96)00289-7) were paywalled. NBS 1942 (10.6028/jres.028.010) was read in full: it gives conductivities only for rubber-CaCO3 composites, plus an OCR-garbled dielectric-constant table (marble about 8.6) that was not entered. The search found no number for the resistivity of pure CaCO3.
- **band_gap_caco3, primary experiment.** Janovec et al. 2025 J Mater Chem C (10.1039/d4tc04921g) is listed as open access, but the publisher refused both the PDF and the HTML route, and the full-text route also failed. Web search snippets indicate it discusses optical gaps of 7.1-7.5 eV, but this could not be read through the tool. Baer and Blanchard 1993 (10.1016/0169-4332(93)90365-i), Hossain et al. 2009 (10.1016/j.ssc.2009.04.026) and the full text of Medeiros et al. 2007 were paywalled or unreachable. Only a quoted 6.0 ± 0.35 eV (TG-019, D) and a crude UV-vis estimate (TG-020, C) were entered.
- **tunnel_barrier_caco3 (electron affinity of CaCO3, or barrier height at a CaCO3/metal or carbonate/Al interface).** The crossref search for calcite electron affinity and work function returned nothing relevant. No CaCO3 or carbonate tunnelling measurement was found. Recorded instead as PROXIES: native AlOx on Al tunnel barriers (TG-021 to TG-025, slug tunnel_barrier_alox_proxy). A web search snippet also pointed to a Nb/AlOx study (arXiv cond-mat/0402092), which was not fetched.
- **tunnel_prefactor through carbonate films.** None was found. The only entry is a grade-C proxy decay constant derived from a fitted Al/AlOx/Al barrier (TG-026). No measured current prefactor or decay constant through a CaCO3 film exists in the sources searched.
- **Route failures.** arXiv search returned HTTP 429 on all retries, and openalex returned HTTP 429 later in the session. The arXiv items were fetched by identifier instead.
