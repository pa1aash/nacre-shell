# T-I gaps

- **Wagman et al. 1982 NBS tables (10.1063/1.555845).** Tried fulltext by DOI (paywalled, no open copy), the srd.nist.gov reprint URL (not retrieved), and the NIST digitized CSV (data DOI 10.18434/M32124; not retrieved by the url route). The values used instead come from the open predecessor NBS Technical Notes 270-3, 270-6 and 270-8, and from USGS Bulletin 2131. Only TN 270-8 (lithium) extracts as readable text.
- **NIST-JANAF 4th edition (Chase 1998).** The open element PDFs on janaf.nist.gov (carbon, calcium) were fetched, but they are image-only and extract no text. No value could be read. CaO and CO2 JANAF values appear only as a grade-C compilation through USGS OFR 83-79.
- **CODATA key values (Cox, Wagman, Medvedev 1989).** No open PDF was found, and the tool has no HTML route. The B2131 aqueous-ion values cite ref. 85 of that report, which was not opened.
- **Decomposition-equilibrium papers (CO2 pressure over calcite).** An openalex search for the CaO-CO2 equilibrium returned kinetic and process papers and USGS OFR 83-79, which is used. Baker 1962 (cited in OFR 83-79) was not searched separately. The report-based reaction values (Robinson et al. 1983) stand in for it.
- **Al(OH)4-(aq) and gibbsite DfG.** Al(OH)4- is not in B2131. The gibbsite DfG in B2131 is garbled by OCR in every extracted copy, so only the gibbsite DfH was recorded. Day 1 TB-032/TB-035 remain the only Al(OH)4- and gibbsite DfG rows. CO2(aq) is recorded only as a derived value from H2CO3 (un-ionized) and H2O(l).
- **Al(NO3)3, Ca(ClO4)2, Al(ClO4)3, AlCl3 thermochemistry.** NBS TN 270-3 (aluminium compounds) and TN 270-6 (calcium compounds) were fetched, but their tables are rotated and the extracted text is unreadable. Wagman 1982 is paywalled, the NIST CSV was not retrievable, and B2131 does not list these salts. Ca(NO3)2, CaCl2, CaSO4, Al2(SO4)3 and the lithium salts are covered (TI-029..TI-049).
- **CaCO3 (or other carbonate-salt) anodic oxidation onset vs Li+/Li.** An openalex search for CaCO3 electrochemical oxidation in non-aqueous electrolyte with CO2 evolution returned no relevant work. Only Li2CO3 rows exist (TI-059..TI-063).
- **Renfrew & McCloskey 2017 and Yang et al. on Li2CO3 in carbonate electrolytes.** Neither surfaced in openalex searches on residual lithium carbonate with CO2 evolution. Freiberg et al. 2020 is abstract-only: the Elsevier copy failed. Rinkel et al. 2020 was read but reports no Li2CO3 onset.
- **Al anodic onset in LiClO4 or LiNO3 electrolytes (independent of TB-067/068).** Kanamura et al. 1995 (10.1016/0378-7753(95)02265-1), Zhang & Jow 2002 (10.1016/s0378-7753(02)00110-6) and Krause et al. 1997 (10.1016/s0378-7753(97)02517-2) are paywalled, with no abstract from any route. Markovsky et al. 2010 (10.1149/1.3294774) is paywalled and its abstract has no numbers. Park et al. 2015 (lithium borates on Al) is paywalled. In the Xie et al. 2008 abstract (LiBOB in polymer electrolyte), all numbers are stripped.
- **Other fluorine-free salts (LiDCTA, LiB(CN)4, LiNO3 in glymes) and solvents (sulfolane, adiponitrile, glymes) with Al.** Openalex searches on dicyanotriazolate and tetracyanoborate with Al returned only reviews and theses with no Al potential. The fluorine-free reviews (10.1002/batt.202100373, 10.1038/s44296-025-00074-8, 10.20517/energymater.2023.53) give only qualitative statements or cite LiBOB work already in Day 1 (TB-060). The trace-water content is recorded for one work only (TI-067).
- **Solvation or transfer free energies of Ca2+ and Al3+ in carbonate or ether solvents.** Marcus 1983 (IUPAC) lists Ca2+ only as tentative or unreliable entries for other solvents, with no PC value, and has no Al3+. Kalidas, Hefter & Marcus 2000 (Chem. Rev.) was not retrieved. Okoshi et al. 2016 (10.1149/2.0211702jes) and Araujo et al. 2021 (10.1016/j.ensm.2021.04.015) gave no retrievable copy. An openalex search for Al3+ solvation in organic solvents returned nothing relevant. Only the cluster free energies of Shakourian-Fard et al. 2019 (with a BF4- counter-ion, no benchmark) are recorded. solvation_free_energy_li_org has no row; only the water to PC transfer free energy for Li+ is recorded.
- **Hautier et al. 2012 full text and Wang, Maxisch & Ceder 2006 numeric O2 correction.** Both are paywalled. An arXiv search returned HTTP 429 three times. Hautier is abstract-only (D). The 1.36 eV O2 shift is recorded as a compilation via Jain et al. 2011.
- **Fitted CO2 (or carbonate) gas-phase correction.** MP2020 (Wang et al. 2021) fits no CO2 or carbonate correction (TI-084 notes). Granda-Marulanda et al. 2020 (10.1021/acscatal.0c01075) gave no retrievable copy. Openalex was rate-limited (429) for the carbonate-correction query. No CO2 correction value is recorded.
