# T-N gaps

Routes used: openalex, crossref, europepmc (full-text queries), arxiv, all logged in queries.jsonl. The semanticscholar route returned HTTP 429 on every retry (one query, 0 results). Web search was used only to discover DOIs (PVDF surface energy, LFP D50, particle imprint into foil, foil roughness).

- **1. PVDF surface free energy (pvdf_surface_energy_total, _dispersive, _polar): covered, two independent film sources.** Found: Szewczyk et al. 2019 spin-coated film, Owens-Wendt with water, glycerol and formamide (TN-001 to TN-003); Wu 1982 as listed in that paper (TN-004 to TN-006, secondary, grade C); Eskhan et al. 2023 NMP-cast film, van Oss with water, formamide and diiodomethane (TN-007 to TN-012, including gamma+ and gamma-). The water contact angle is TN-012 (78.9 deg, Eskhan); Szewczyk shows the film water angles only in a figure. Functionalised PVDF latices (TN-013 to TN-015) are a proxy. The two film sources disagree on the polar part (7.20 against 0.00 mJ/m2). The methods partition differently (OW with three polar liquids against vOCG), so the split depends on the method. Not found: a PVDF-water interfacial energy as a printed number, and any IGC result on PVDF (the KAKEN project record lists PVDF but gives no number and has no DOI). Excluded: the electrospun-membrane value of 10.3390/membranes11090690 (5.8 mJ/m2), which is an apparent value on a porous rough surface (Cassie regime), not a film property. Not retrieved: Weber et al. 2024 (10.1149/1945-7111/ad3a24, no retrievable copy), the origin of some Table 1 values in 10.1038/s41598-025-93813-9. Queries: openalex "polyvinylidene fluoride film surface free energy dispersive polar components contact angle Owens-Wendt", "poly(vinylidene fluoride) surface energy Lifshitz-van der Waals acid-base van Oss contact angle film", "XDLVO PVDF membrane surface tension components ...", "PVDF binder surface energy ..."; europepmc PVDF + surface free energy + Owens (open access); crossref "surface free energy of PVDF film contact angle dispersive polar component".

Not found: an open, printed D50 of a commercial LFP powder or of a specific commercial NCA powder. Raffenberg et al. 2026 (10.1016/j.est.2026.121051; five LFP grades) had no retrievable copy, and its abstract gives no sizes. The companion work 10.1016/j.jpowsour.2026.241057 had no retrievable copy. Supplier datasheets found by web search (Sigma-Aldrich 939420, MTI LIB-LFPO) have no DOI and were not used. - **5. ca_ionic_radius: covered via an open compilation; ca_coordination_number: single source for water.** Found: Shannon 1976 Ca2+ radii for CN 6 to 9 as tabulated by Lundberg, Warminska, Fuchs and Persson 2018, PCCP (TN-035 to TN-038, C, Shannon named as original), and the same paper's own crystal-structure survey radii for CN 6 to 8 (TN-039 to TN-041, derived). - **6. water_activity_drying_primary: partial (dew point, not activity).** Found: Kumberg et al. 2019, aqueous graphite/CMC/SBR anode drying. Ambient dew point 15 C for free-convection drying (TN-043), dew points 15-30 C used in drying-rate calculations (TN-044), isothermal film temperature 31-49 C in the constant-rate period (TN-045), and a statement that the film sits at the steady-state temperature during the constant-rate period (TN-046, qualitative). Not found: a water activity or relative humidity printed at the film surface, or a drying-air RH, for an aqueous cathode on Al during convective drying. - **7. input_correlation: not found for any group.** Recorded per group:
  - *cnt_fit*: Hamm et al. 2014 (10.1073/pnas.1312369111) gives no covariance between gamma and ln A. Its "correlation" is the physical link between gamma and the binding free energy. Nielsen et al. 2020 (10.1021/acs.cgd.0c00029) gives only standard errors of B and ln A (Table 1, e.g. carboxyl SAM B = 397 ± 65, ln A = 20 ± 2) and no covariance. Since both come from a single linear fit of ln J0 against 1/sigma^2, they are correlated by construction, but no value is printed. Giuffre et al. 2013 (10.1073/pnas.1222162110) had no retrievable copy. Li et al. 2014 ES&T (10.1021/es405141j) is paywalled.
  - *carbonate_system*: Orr et al. 2018 (10.1016/j.marchem.2018.10.006, uncertainty propagation for the CO2 system) was found with open copies (HAL, ScienceDirect accepted manuscript). Both `fulltext` and `url` crashed in PDF page-label extraction (pymupdf get_label_pno IndexError), so it was not read; this is an instrument failure that may not have been logged automatically. Humphreys et al. 2024 (10.5194/os-20-1325-2024) prints a covariance only between fitted coefficients of the fCO2 temperature dependence, not between pK1, pK2 or Ksp; not recorded.
  - *solubility, caco3_decomposition, young_energies*: no source printing a correlation coefficient or covariance between Ksp values, CaCO3/CaO/CO2 formation values or Young-equation energies was found.
