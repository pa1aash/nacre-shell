# T-J gaps

- **al_ocp_alkaline in a cathode slurry or at pH 10-12.** Searched openalex, crossref and europepmc (Al current collector open-circuit potential in aqueous cathode slurry; open-circuit potential of aluminium vs pH in alkaline solution; Al in LiOH solution; Al in weakly alkaline solution), plus a web search for slurry-specific values. Found instead: mixed potentials of Al in 1 M NaOH (TJ-023), in 4 M NaOH (TJ-024) and of 6061 alloy in glycol-water at pH 8.8 (TJ-025). These bracket the range but none is a cathode slurry. Not usable: Li et al. 2016 Mater. Corros. (10.1002/maco.201608843) is paywalled and its OSTI copy timed out. Bauer et al. 2019 Electrochim. Acta (10.1016/j.electacta.2019.05.141): the KIT repository copy timed out twice and the archive returned 404. 10.1007/s10800-017-1081-2 (Al in LiNO3/Li2SO4 vs Ag/AgCl) and Hurlen 1984 (10.1016/0013-4686(84)87167-4) are paywalled with no abstract value. The source papers do not state the Hg/HgO filling for TJ-023 and TJ-024, so the 1 M NaOH or 1 M KOH offset (TJ-027, TJ-028) cannot be assigned with certainty.
- **Ag/AgCl offset.** No source used here reports a potential vs Ag/AgCl, so no Ag/AgCl offset was fetched.
- **slurry_temperature during mixing.** Most works state only room or ambient temperature; one says explicitly that it had no active temperature control (10.3390/appliedchem5040033). Only two set temperatures were found: binder dissolution at 40 degC (TJ-056) and a leaching suspension at 25 ± 2 degC (TJ-081). No source reports the temperature rise during high-shear mixing.
- **water_activity during drying.** Searched crossref and europepmc (residual moisture in water-based cathodes, Karl Fischer, drying). No measured or modelled water activity of a drying aqueous cathode coating was found. Found instead: residual water after secondary drying (TJ-054, TJ-055, one source) and solids content, which gives the water mass fraction (40-63 wt% water). Huttner et al. 2020 Energy Technol. (10.1002/ente.201900245, post-drying and residual water) is paywalled.
- **slurry_conductivity.** Searched crossref (aqueous cathode slurry conductivity, supernatant, lithium leaching) and ran a web search. No measured conductivity of a slurry or its supernatant was found. The ionic strength is bounded only through derived rows from printed Li+ filtrate concentrations (TJ-104, TJ-105, LiOH 1:1 assumption).
- **slurry_ph with CO2 bubbling.** The only CO2-treatment source found was a patent with no DOI, which cannot be read through the tool, and no peer-reviewed open work. Acid-modified slurries already in Day 1 (H3PO4, PAA, acetic acid) were not re-entered. Loeffler et al. 2016 (10.1002/cssc.201600353, H3PO4 in NMC111) gives no pH in its abstract and is paywalled. Cetinel and Bauer 2014 (10.1007/s12034-014-0733-7, pH vs time of NMC111 pastes) and Bichon et al. 2019 (10.1021/acsami.9b00999) are paywalled. The unmodified pH of an LFP slurry was not found in an open source; only the acid-adjusted value of 6.3 was (TJ-088).
- **incell_potential for NMC532, LMO and NCA as separate works.** NMC532 appears only in an abstract (10.1149/1945-7111/ada641) and without a cut-off. No new LMO cut-off was found beyond Day 1 TA-052. NCA is covered only inside the joint 4.3 V row TJ-036.
- **al_onset_potential in LiBOB or other fluorine-free salts beyond Day 1.** Morita et al. 2002 (10.1016/s0013-4686(02)00164-0) and Kanamura 1999 (10.1016/s0378-7753(99)00141-x) are paywalled with no abstract. The Krämer et al. 2013 abstract (10.1149/2.081302jes, LiTFSI) gives no onset value. The ECS meeting abstract on LiClO4 with added water (Al up to 5 V with water below 50 ppm) has no DOI and was not read. The only fluorine-free onset found in a full text is LiClO4-PC from Suresh et al. 2002 (TJ-031, TJ-032).
- **electrolyte_water_content in fluorine-free cells.** One Karl-Fischer specification was found (TJ-048, LiTFSI and LiPF6 electrolytes). Suresh et al. give only the dry-box moisture (TJ-049).
- **nucleation_prefactor_j0, other originals.** Five originals could not be read:
  - Giuffre et al. 2013 PNAS (10.1073/pnas.1222162110): PMC full text failed over four routes. The abstract has no J0.
  - Sohnel and Mullin 1982, Verdoes and Kashchiev 1992 and Kashchiev and van Rosmalen 2003: paywalled. Two had no abstract and one abstract had no values. Their J0 range enters only through the compilation row TJ-022.
  - Nielsen et al. 2014 Science (10.1126/science.1254051) and 10.1021/acs.jpcc.9b00378: paywalled.
  - Knight et al. 2024 (10.1021/acs.cgd.4c00602): ln A values are only in SI Table S4, and the SI PDF was not retrieved. The main text gives only a 5-45x increase with sulfation.

  Li et al. 2014 and Fernandez-Martinez et al. 2013 (ES&T) are paywalled, and their abstracts give interfacial energies but not J0.
- **nucleation_density on organic templates.** Hamm et al. (Fig. 1, nuclei per 0.3072 mm2), Hu et al. (Fig. 3 SEM) and Li and Jun (Fig. 5 AFM counts per um2) report counts only graphically, and no number is printed in the text. Aizenberg et al. 1999 Nature (10.1038/19047) is paywalled. Searches for PDA- or polysaccharide-templated CaCO3 densities (openalex, europepmc) returned calcium phosphate or qualitative studies only (10.1038/s41598-020-71900-3, 10.1038/s41467-023-43608-1).
- **induction_time on templates at stated supersaturation.** None is printed in the fetched template studies. They report steady-state rates instead (TJ-007, TJ-017) or CNT barriers in kT (TJ-010 to TJ-012). Koishi et al. 2019 (10.1021/acs.chemmater.9b00417) derives induction times on micas only in its SI. The Hu et al. 75 s observation refers to particles on bare gold and was not entered.
