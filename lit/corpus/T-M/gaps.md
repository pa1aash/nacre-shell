# T-M gaps

- **Catechol pKa at I = 0 (thermodynamic) and with a stated uncertainty from an open primary source.** Open primaries found are at I = 0.1 (TM-001 to TM-006) and, via a compilation, I = 0.15 M KCl (TM-007/008); none extrapolates to I = 0. Paywalled or no open copy: Charkoudian & Franz 2006 (10.1021/ic060014r), Mirzahosseini et al. 2018 (10.1016/j.jpba.2018.06.015), Radalla et al. 2015 (10.1016/j.bjbas.2015.05.012). Checked but no catechol constant of their own: Turkel et al. 2004 (10.1248/cpb.52.929), Merce et al. 2005 (10.1590/s0103-50532005000100006, catechol only from a compilation), Malacaria et al. 2023 (10.3390/app13020776), Palla et al. 2024 (10.3390/ijms252312727, catecholamines only). Queries: openalex "catechol protonation constants potentiometric ionic strength 0.1 M KCl 25 C", "pyrocatechol acid dissociation constants spectrophotometric ionic strength", "catechol ionization constant temperature dependence aqueous potentiometric thermodynamic"; europepmc catechol protonation constants with OPEN_ACCESS; crossref "acid dissociation constants of catechol in aqueous solution", Acta Chem Scand catechol series (the Si/Al series parts found were hydrolysis, salicylate and polysilicate, not catechol).
