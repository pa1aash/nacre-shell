# T-Q gaps

- **resistivity_caco3 (electronic resistivity of calcite or compact CaCO3): no value found.** Searched: openalex "electrical conductivity of calcite high temperature impedance spectroscopy"; crossref "calcite single crystal dc electrical resistivity room temperature dielectric loss insulator" (no relevant hits); a web search for mineral resistivity tables. Ter Heege & Renner 2007 (10.1007/s00269-007-0162-x) is paywalled and no abstract came back (Semantic Scholar 429). Bagdassarov & Slutskii 2003 (10.1080/0141159031000098233) is paywalled; its abstract gives only conductivity activation energies at 0.5-2.5 GPa and 600-1200 C (TQ-030, TQ-031, D). Zhao et al. 2026 GRL (10.1029/2025gl119568, open) gives the conduction mechanism: conduction in crystalline CaCO3 is ionic (TQ-032). It prints numbers only for molten and amorphous CaCO3 at 6-15 GPa. A textbook mineral-resistivity table (Telford-type) showed up only as a course-site copy with no DOI or licence, so it was not used. No ambient-temperature resistivity of pure CaCO3 exists in any open source found. Physics: any impedance value is ionic plus electronic, so it is only an upper bound on electronic conduction.
- **electron_affinity_caco3 (calcite CBM vs vacuum): no value found.** The openalex search "calcite band alignment ionization potential electron affinity vacuum level" returned nothing relevant. No UPS/IPES or slab-DFT band alignment of calcite was found. The closest carbonate analog is a PROXY: Li2CO3(001) PBE work function 6.02 eV and gap 4.75 eV, giving a barrier of 1.78 eV against Li (TQ-026..TQ-028, Lin et al. 2016). Janovec et al. 2025 (10.1039/d4tc04921g, CC BY-NC) failed again through fulltext, through the direct RSC articlepdf URL, and through an arXiv search for a preprint. Earlier sessions also failed on it.
- **work_function_al:** found. These are UHV experimental values for clean faces, from two sets that disagree on face ordering: 4.24/4.41/4.28 eV (ref. 43) and 4.26/4.20/4.06 eV (ref. 44). Both are quoted in Faleev et al. (TQ-013..TQ-018, C), along with QSGW 4.17/4.36/4.19 eV (TQ-019..TQ-021, B). The primary compilations are paywalled with no open copy: Derry et al. 2015 recommended values (10.1116/1.4934685, abstract only, no numbers) and Michaelson 1977 (10.1063/1.323539). Note that in-cell Al is covered by oxide, and the effective barrier at Al/AlOx is the TG-021..TG-025 proxy.
- **band_gap_caco3, high-level computation or a new primary experiment:** Ulian et al. 2021 (10.1039/d1cp02673a, open postprint) gives PBE 5.15, B3LYP 7.42 and PBE0 8.01 eV (TQ-023..TQ-025). It quotes the 6.0 +/- 0.35 eV experiment (TQ-022), which is the same Baer & Blanchard 1993 measurement as TG-019 and is not an independent source. No GW/BSE gap was retrievable (Janovec 2025, above). Baer & Blanchard 1993 (10.1016/0169-4332(93)90365-i) was paywalled in an earlier session and was not retried.
- **effective_mass_caco3: no value found.** Searched: arXiv "calcite CaCO3 electronic structure band gap GW hybrid functional", openalex "calcite aragonite vaterite band structure effective mass density functional", crossref "calcium carbonate calcite carrier effective mass electrons holes first-principles", and a web search. None prints a calcite electron effective mass. An Authorea preprint on CaCO3 hydrates (10.22541/au.157660447.71970169) was fetched, but no relevant text was extracted. The only tunnelling model for a carbonate (Lin 2016, TQ-029) uses the free electron mass.
- **tunnel_prefactor_j0 for a carbonate or comparable wide-gap ionic film: not found.** Lin et al. 2016 computes only a WKB transmission with a blocking criterion of T = e^-40 (d* = 30.2 angstrom for Li2CO3, TQ-029), with no current prefactor. Fang et al. 2026 (10.1038/s41524-026-02362-w) gives electron-transfer resistances (qualitative ranking, TQ-033), not a Simmons-type J0. The existing proxy remains TG-026 (AlOx decay constant).
