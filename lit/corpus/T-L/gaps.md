# T-L gaps

- **surface_energy_calcite_dry: no experimental free energy of calcite against vacuum or dry gas.** OpenAlex and arXiv searches covered DFT surface energies, cleavage experiments, contact-angle component methods and inverse gas chromatography. Experiments give only the dispersive component (TL-025, TL-026, TL-028 to TL-030) and a lower bound from water spreading (TL-027). The cleavage studies Gupta and Santhanam 1969 (10.1016/0001-6160(69)90022-4) and Røyne et al. 2011 (10.1029/2010jb008033, no retrievable copy) were not read. Every vacuum value in the rows is computed, and only one source is a primary computation: Onawole et al. 2020, PBE-D3. Bruno et al. 2013 adds a 300 K free energy (TL-018) that rests on an estimated vibrational term. The primary DFT and force-field papers are paywalled or have no open copy: Akiyama et al. (Phys. Rev. B), de Leeuw and Parker 1998, Raiteri et al. 2010 (10.1021/jp910977a), Ataman et al. 2016 (10.1021/acs.jpcc.6b01359), Ataman et al. 2026 (10.1021/acs.langmuir.6c04191) and 10.1021/jp0441617. Zhou et al. 2024 (arXiv 2408.12222) prints no surface energies. No calcite IGC run on single crystals was found. The calorimetric surface enthalpy of Forbes et al. 2011 is already TC-016 as a secondary row; the original was not re-fetched.
- **surface_energy_acc_dry / surface_enthalpy_acc: not found.** Searches (OpenAlex, Crossref, web discovery) for ACC surface energy, surface enthalpy, nanoparticle calorimetry and vacuum simulations of ACC returned nothing quantitative. Radha et al. 2010 PNAS (10.1073/pnas.1009959107) states that ACC surface energies were unknown (TL-031). Note: the DOI in the brief (10.1073/pnas.1003082107) resolves to no record; the correct DOI is 10.1073/pnas.1009959107. Innocenti Malini et al. 2017 (ACC-water interface MD) concerns the wet interface and was not fetched.
- **log10_k_caoh: no primary determination read.** Both rows (TL-032 from WATEQ4F, TL-033 from phreeqc.dat) trace to Nordstrom et al. 1990, so they are not independent. Plummer and Busenberg 1982 (GCA 46, 1011) and Nordstrom et al. 1990 (ACS Symp. Ser. 416) were not fetched (paywalled). The only open measurement found is a conditional association constant at I = 1 M (TL-034).
- **debye_huckel_a printed directly as A_gamma (log10 basis) at 15-45 C: not found.** Archer and Wang 1990 (open NIST reprint) and Fernández et al. 1997 tabulate only A_phi, so the A rows are derived (grade C). The only direct printed log10 A is the llnl.dat grid value at 25 C (TL-046). Helgeson and Kirkham 1974 was not fetched. The PHREEQC 3 manual's equations did not survive text extraction (they are images in the PDF).
- **davies_b and davies_validity_ionic_strength: original not read.** Davies 1938 (10.1039/JR9380002093) and Davies 1962 (book) were not fetched (paywalled or not in an open route). Every Davies row is secondary (grade C). The validity statements are either attributed (Davies 1938, Zeebe and Wolf-Gladrow 2001) or apply to the whole WATEQ4F ion-association model. No open source quantifies the Davies error against the I = 0.5 mol/kg limit.
- **water_activity_drying during slurry or film drying (per drying stage): not found.** Searches covered electrode drying humidity and dew point, latex and particle film drying water activity, and aqueous cathode slurry drying. The water activities found belong to vacuum post-drying and the dry room, computed from dew points (TL-063, TL-064). Residual water contents are recorded under a separate slug (TL-068 to TL-071). Bryntesen et al. 2021 (10.3390/en14051406) gives a dry-room dew point whose sign did not survive extraction, so no row was made. Wood et al. 2017 (10.1080/07373937.2017.1319855) gives drying-energy analysis without gas humidity. No water activity of a drying aqueous film by stage (constant-rate, falling-rate, pore-emptying) was found.
- **site_footprint_area and ca_headgroup_ratio: not found.** Searches covered COOH-terminated alkanethiol SAMs on Au(111) (area per molecule) and Ca2+ binding stoichiometry at fatty-acid Langmuir monolayers. Jadhav 2011 (10.2478/s11532-011-0024-8) prints no area. Vericat et al. 2006 (10.1088/0953-8984/18/48/r01) and Love et al. 2005 were not open. The open values found were in patents (no DOI), so they were not used. Brezesinski et al. 2019 (10.1021/acs.langmuir.9b00191) covers the X-ray fluorescence method on sulfate monolayers, not Ca-carboxylate stoichiometry. Sthoer et al. 2021 (La3+, Y3+) was not fetched.
- **pda_catechol_content (catechol or quinone per mass) and any printed rho_L: not found.** The only proxy is accessible amine-reactive sites by PTEA uptake (TL-076, slug pda_amine_binding_site_content). Herman et al. 2023 (neutron reflectometry of PDA films, 10.1016/j.polymer.2023.126272) had no retrievable copy. A directly measured film density was not found; the density rows are a precipitate (1.2 g/cm3), literature melanin values and a nominal statement.
- **template_layer_thickness under the standard protocol on oxide or metal versus time: partial.** The rows are on silicon or QCM sensors (Bernsmann thesis) and on Cr with persulfate (Cotroneo). Lee et al. 2007 (Science) and Ball et al. 2012 are paywalled. Ball 2024 (10.1515/ntrev-2023-0216) failed mid-download (connection broken), and Ball 2023 (10.1002/marc.202200946) timed out on the open repository host. No COOH SAM thickness was found.
- **ca_ionic_radius: not found.** Shannon 1976 (10.1107/S0567739476001551) is paywalled with no open copy. The two Persson reviews fetched give Ca-O distances, not ionic radii. Jalilehvand et al. 2001 (10.1021/ja001533a) is paywalled, so the 2.46 A LAXS distance is read only as a secondary citation (Persson 2024).
- **contact_patch_area: not found.** Searched calendering imprint and indentation of cathode particles into aluminium foil, and contact areas between particles and the collector from cross-sections or contact mechanics (OpenAlex, two queries). The finite-element calendering study (arXiv 2601.02879) models particle fracture against silicon plates, not foil contact. Only a D50 fallback row was recorded (cathode_particle_d50, supplier value).
- **adhesion_free_energy_method_error (absolute free-energy errors): not found as such.** The INTERFACE force-field silica paper (Emami et al. 2014) gives immersion-enthalpy agreement and contact-angle agreement (±3 deg), recorded as proxies, plus a general <5% claim. Heinz et al. 2013 (10.1021/la3038846) is paywalled. The interfacial free-energy review of Yang et al. 2025 (10.1016/j.cis.2025.103482) was read but prints no quantitative method errors. No PMF or contact-angle study of oxides with an absolute work-of-adhesion error in mJ/m2 was retrieved.
- **spreading_coefficient_method_error: not found.** No computed mineral- or organic-water interfacial free energy was found with an experiment-referenced absolute error. The only evidence is the spread of force-field calcite/water values (0.090 to 0.387 J/m2, Bruno et al. 2013) and the force-field against DFT difference for the dry (10-14) energy. Both are spreads, not errors.
