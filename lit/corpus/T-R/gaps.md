- **Patents (no DOI, so no rows under the schema).** Read from patents.google.com text. US 10,243,206 (filed 2017-05-16) and US 10,734,641 (filed 2019-02-25; same title, assignee Battelle Memorial Institute; priority 2016-05-17), "High capacity and stable cathode materials": Li3PO4 deposited by ALD (LiOtBu and trimethylphosphate, 300 C, 150 cycles, about 10 nm) on LiNi0.80Mn0.10Co0.10O2 and LiNi0.76Mn0.14Co0.10O2, annealed at 600 C for 2 h; coin cells cycled 2.7 to 4.5 V vs Li/Li+ (charge cutoff 4.5 V), C/3 after three C/10 formation cycles, room temperature and 60 C, 200 cycles; the electrolyte is not named in the examples read. Evidence class B for Li3PO4 at a 4.5 V cutoff. The patent reports 91.6 percent retention for the infused material against 79.0 percent (pristine) at room temperature, and 73.2 against 58.3 percent at 60 C; these are device-level figures and are kept out of the table. These two patents are the only tier-2 sources here with a 4.5 V cutoff stated in text.
