# T-F gaps

- **Calcite single-crystal refinement as primary (molar_volume_calcite).** Searched OpenAlex for calcite single-crystal structure refinements and reference lattice parameters, and requested full text for Maslen et al. 1993 (10.1107/s0108768193002575) and Effenberger et al. 1981 (10.1524/zkri.1981.156.3-4.233). Both are paywalled with no open copy. Ishizawa et al. 2013 (10.1038/srep02832) is open, but its room-temperature cell is only in SI tables that were not reachable. Lang et al. 2020 (10.1002/chem.202003344) has its lattice parameters in SI Table S1, also unread. Markgraf and Reeder 1985 and Graf 1961 (American Mineralogist) have no DOI, so they could not be logged. What was found instead: the Robie et al. 1967 compilation of Graf 1961 (TF-001 to TF-004), and a primary neutron refinement of biogenic otolith calcite (TF-005 to TF-008). No row comes from a single-crystal refinement of pure calcite.
- **Vaterite molar volume from a modern structure model (molar_volume_vaterite).** Chakoumakos et al. 2016 (10.1038/srep36799) prints the P6522 cell volume but not Z, so no molar volume was derived (TF-013). San et al. 2023 (10.1038/s41467-023-43625-0) prints approximate monoclinic cell edges only, with no Z or density. Mugnaioli et al. 2012 (10.1002/anie.201200845) was not fetched. The only vaterite molar volume recorded is the 1967 compilation of McConnell 1960 (TF-011).
- **Ca2+ binding to unsubstituted catechol, 4-methylcatechol, L-DOPA or a catechol polymer (logk_ca_catecholate).** Searches: OpenAlex for alkaline-earth catecholate formation constants and for Ca/Mg catechol stability constants; Crossref for Ca and Mg complexes of catecholamines and for the Gergely/Kiss series "Complexes of 3,4-dihydroxyphenyl derivatives"; Europe PMC for catechol-calcium binding constants; and web discovery for Ca-catechol, caffeic acid and protocatechuic acid constants. Results:
  - The IUPAC critical survey for L-DOPA (Pettit 1984, 10.1351/pac198456020247) has no Ca(II) entry.
  - Lebedev et al. 2007 (10.1002/cphc.200700296) is paywalled. Its abstract only says that Ca2+-catecholate complexes form (TF-017).
  - The Erdogan et al. 2005 quercetin-Ca study (10.1515/revac.2005.24.1.9) had no retrievable copy, and its abstract has no numbers.
  - Irto et al. 2023 (10.3389/fchem.2023.1189308) and Aiello et al. 2024 (10.3390/inorganics13010002) report no Ca2+ constants.
  - The Gergely/Kiss papers are paywalled journal articles.
  
  What was found instead: Ca2+-Tiron constants (TF-014 to TF-016). Tiron is a sulfonated catecholate, so this is a proxy. TD-048/TD-049 (dopamine) remain the only Ca-catecholamine determinations.
- **Primary Ca2+-monocarboxylate constants and an IUPAC critical evaluation (logk_ca_carboxylate).** Searches: OpenAlex for Ca acetate formation constants, conductometric Ca acetate/propionate association, Ca malonate/succinate constants, and ITC of Ca-polyacrylate; web discovery for the Ca-acetate ion pair. Results:
  - Bunting and Thong 1970 (10.1139/v70-273) and Antonilli et al. 2009 (10.3184/095422909x418502) had no retrievable copy.
  - Gindele et al. 2022 (10.1021/acs.langmuir.2c01662) is paywalled.
  - Shehata et al. 1994 (10.1039/ft9949003401) appeared in searches but is paywalled at RSC; its full text was not requested.
  - Daniele et al. 2008 (Coord. Chem. Rev., compiled in TD-051) is paywalled.
  - Mantha et al. 2024 (10.1021/acs.langmuir.3c03640) gives only MD decoupling free energies, not a binding constant.
  
  What was found instead: Martell-Smith values for acetate and succinate, read through a secondary source (TF-019, TF-020), and an ITC apparent constant for Ca2+-PAA (TF-018). No open primary measurement for a simple Ca2+-carboxylate was read.
- **Potentiometric primary pKa of catechol (pka_catechol).** Jameson and Wilson 1972 (10.1039/dt9720002610) is paywalled, and its abstract gives no numbers. A search for potentiometric catechol protonation constants at different ionic strengths returned one unrelated hit. The value recorded instead is a spectrophotometric determination at uncontrolled ionic strength (TF-021, TF-022), plus the literature range that work compiles (TF-025).
- **Surface pKa of COOH-terminated monolayers (pka_carboxyl).** Searched OpenAlex for the surface pKa of carboxylic-acid-terminated SAMs. Hu et al. 1997 (10.1021/la9700782) is paywalled. Santiago et al. 2018 (10.1021/acs.jpcc.7b11878) had no retrievable copy. Nagasawa 1971 (10.1351/pac197126030519) was read, but no tabulated intrinsic pK0 for PAA could be identified in its text layer. Recorded instead: solution values for acetic and succinic acid, PAA and propionic acid (TF-026 to TF-030). No interfacial carboxyl pKa was read.
- **Slurry Ca concentration (slurry_ca).** Searched OpenAlex for Ca2+ additives in aqueous cathode slurries and for Ca-crosslinked alginate binders in cathodes, Crossref for Ca2+-crosslinked alginate binders in Li-ion electrodes, and Europe PMC for Ca content of CMC binders. Rolandi et al. 2024 (10.1039/d4su00098f), a review of aqueous processing and water-soluble binders, contains no mention of calcium. No Ca concentration in an aqueous cathode slurry, binder solution or leachate was found. In this search Ca appears only as something the design adds, so the screen must treat slurry Ca as a design input (the Ca dosed to form the layer), not a measured property of the slurry.
- **Dissolved inorganic carbon in an air-exposed or CO2-treated slurry (slurry_dic).**
  - Kimura et al. 2018 (10.1149/2.0241802jes) reports the pH drop from CO2 treatment and Li2CO3 formation, but no DIC concentration (TF-033 to TF-035).
  - Pritzl et al. 2019 (10.1149/2.1351915jes) measured carbonate under argon only, which excludes CO2 uptake (TF-031, TF-032).
  
  No measured DIC was found for a slurry processed in air.
- **Catechol or PDA surface functional-group density, and alginate or chitosan group densities (site_density_organic).** Searched OpenAlex for quantified amine and catechol group densities on polydopamine coatings, and web discovery for PDA catechol density from XPS or titration. No numeric catechol, amine or carboxyl site density on PDA, alginate or chitosan films was found. Only COOH-terminated SAM densities were recorded (TF-036 to TF-038).
- **Plummer and Busenberg 1982 original (carbonate constants, CaHCO3+, CaCO3(aq)).** A full-text request for 10.1016/0016-7037(82)90056-4 returned paywalled, with no open copy. Recorded instead: the WATEQ4F restatement (TF-041 to TF-047) and the independent Goldberg et al. 2002 evaluation of pK1 and pK2 (TF-039, TF-040). There is no second independent source for log K(CaHCO3+) or log K(CaCO3 aq); both rows trace to the same original. No numeric Davies-equation constant was read; WATEQ4F only states that the Davies equation is used for species without Debye-Huckel parameters.
- **Water density from IAPWS-95 or a metrological table (water_density).** The Wagner and Pruss 2002 reprint (10.1063/1.1461829) at the NIST reprint location could not be retrieved, and Tanaka et al. 2001 (10.1088/0026-1394/38/4/3) is paywalled. Recorded instead: the Jones and Harris 1992 ITS-90 refit of the Kell 1975 formulation (TF-049).
- **Second source for the surface tension of water (gamma_water).** Only the IAPS table of Vargaftik et al. 1983 was read (TF-048). No open independent primary measurement at 25 C was searched for separately within this thread.
- **Ca2+ hydration free energy determined directly on the cluster-pair scale (hydration_free_energy_ca).**
  - Tissandier et al. 1998 (10.1021/jp982638r) is paywalled.
  - The author's copy of Kelly et al. 2006 (10.1021/jp063552y) was read, but it contains no Ca2+ entry (monovalent ions only).
  - Asthagiri et al. 2003 (arXiv physics/0303062) and Tazi et al. 2012 (arXiv 1204.2084) give no absolute Ca2+ value.
  - A PCCP 2021 article on proton hydration (10.1039/d1cp01454d) has no Ca2+ value.
  
  Recorded instead: the cluster-pair proton value (TF-050) and a rescaling of the Marcus Ca2+ value to that proton reference (TF-051). This is derived from the same Marcus data, so it is not independent.
