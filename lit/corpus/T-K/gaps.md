# T-K gaps

- **transfer_free_energy_ca (water to PC, EC, EC:DMC/EC:EMC, DMC, ethyl isopropyl sulfone): no value found.** Marcus 1983 (10.1351/pac198355060977) lists Ca2+ only in parentheses, meaning no value could be selected (index of ions, p. 981). The solvents are D2O, formic acid, ammonia, hydrazine and acetonitrile. The ammonia and hydrazine entries carry zero weight. There is one acetic-acid entry, and acetic acid is protic. Acetonitrile has no Ca2+ number in the compiled table (pp. 1010-1011). Gritzner 1988 (10.1351/pac198860121743) covers Ba2+ as its only alkaline-earth ion. Gritzner et al. 1990 (10.1351/pac199062091839, polarographic half-wave potentials) also has no Ca2+. Gritzner 1995 (10.1039/ft9959103843) covers Cu, Zn, Cd, Ba and Pb only, and only its abstract is open. Kalidas, Hefter & Marcus 2000 (10.1021/cr980144k) is paywalled and covers aqueous-organic mixtures. Marcus 1990 (10.1351/pac199062050899) covers aqueous alcohols only. The computational Ca2+ papers report whole-salt solvation energies in implicit solvent (10.26434/chemrxiv-2021-djssr) or cluster energies with a counter-anion (TI-078). None gives a single-ion transfer value. No aprotic-solvent analog (DMSO, DMF, MeCN) with a number was found in open sources. Queries are in queries.jsonl: Ca2+ transfer to PC, DMSO, MeCN and DMF; alkaline-earth single-ion transfer; Ca2+ desolvation in EC/PC by DFT. Semantic Scholar searches returned HTTP 429 three times.
- **transfer_free_energy_al (any carbonate or aprotic solvent): no value found.** Al3+ is absent from the ion index of Marcus 1983 and from the Gritzner 1988 and 1990 compilations. Al3+ is not polarographically reducible in these solvents, so the bis(biphenyl)chromium route gives nothing. Queries tried: "Gibbs energy of transfer Al3+ aluminium ion nonaqueous solvent" (openalex), "aluminium(III) ion transfer Gibbs energy water to organic solvent" (crossref, semanticscholar 429) and "aluminium ion solvation free energy propylene carbonate density functional" (crossref). None returned a measured or computed single-ion value.
- **transfer_free_energy_li beyond PC:** no Li+ value in EC (the Marcus 1983 EC entry at 40 C lists no Li+), DMC, EC:DMC/EC:EMC or ethyl isopropyl sulfone. The PC value is TI-077, and rows TK-004, TK-005 and TK-006 give its reading. The only open source for EC is a ferrocene-based study with no Li+ value.
- **hydration_free_energy_li/ca/al from the original:** Marcus 1991 (10.1039/ft9918702995) is paywalled. Rows TK-001 to TK-003 quote it through a secondary source (grade D).
- **standard_entropy of anhydrous Ca(ClO4)2, and of anhydrous Mg, Sr and Ba perchlorates:** the open tables give no measurement. NBS TN 270-6 tabulates only DfH for all four crystalline perchlorates. It does give S0 for Ca(ClO4)2.4H2O (TK-023), Mg(ClO4)2.6H2O (TK-022) and the aqueous standard states. USGS B2131 has no perchlorates. The NIST-JANAF chlorine section has LiClO4(cr) and NaClO4(cr), but the LiClO4 S0 is an estimate (TK-029). No calorimetric heat-capacity measurement of calcium perchlorate, anhydrous or hydrated, was found. Queries: "calcium perchlorate heat capacity calorimetry entropy" (openalex) and "calcium perchlorate tetrahydrate low-temperature heat capacity thermodynamic properties" (crossref). They returned only other perchlorates or unrelated salts. The TN 270-6 hydrate S0 has no cited measurement on the page.
- **standard_entropy of crystalline LiClO4 with measured basis:** TN 270-8 gives no crystal S0. JANAF gives only an estimate by comparison with KClO4. The direct JANAF text and per-table PDF links (janaf.nist.gov/tables/Cl-046.txt and .pdf) could not be retrieved: not a PDF, HTTP 404. The value was read from the open chlorine-section PDF of the fourth edition.
- **NBS 1982 tables (J. Phys. Chem. Ref. Data 11, Suppl. 2):** not fetched. The TN 270-6 and 270-8 values and their convention statements (TK-030, TK-031) were used instead, as instructed.
- **li2co3_oxidation_onset in carbonate electrolytes by gas analysis, open source:** Freiberg et al. 2020 (10.1016/j.electacta.2020.136271) has no retrievable open copy, so it stays abstract-only (TI-063). Streich et al. 2016 (10.1149/2.0801606jes, open) discusses chemical Li2CO3 decomposition by LiPF6/PF5 but prints no onset potential. Rinkel et al. 2020 (10.1021/jacs.0c06363) and the 2024 carbonate-oxidation paper (10.1021/acselectrochem.4c00106) give no Li2CO3 onset. Sim et al. 2024 (10.1002/aenm.202303985) could not be retrieved. The only carbonate-electrolyte number is Wang et al. 2022, Fig. 5a (TK-064): CO2 evolves above 4.3 V by a chemical route.
- **li2co3_oxidation_overpotential as a number:** neither Mahne 2018 nor Wang 2022 prints a number. Both state the onset is close to or consistent with 3.82 V (TK-060, TK-063, qualitative).
- **charge_cutoff_potential, conventional LiCoO2 limit (4.2 V) from a primary half-cell study:** the open LCO sources found are high-voltage studies (4.5 to 4.6 V; TK-044, TK-047, TK-048) plus one rate-test protocol at 4.3 V (TK-049). ## PH-W1.9 (2026-10-24)
- **Solubility of LiClO4 in propylene carbonate at about 25 C (saturation concentration, mol dm^-3 or mol kg^-1): gap, no value, no scale.** Searched: openalex, crossref and europepmc with about a dozen formulations (solubility, phase diagram, solvate, saturated solution, organic-solvent solubility tables, Debye-Hueckel and Pitzer treatments), two web searches; Semantic Scholar answered HTTP 429 on every try (not a source of any attempt below). Where the number is, and what each attempt returned:
  - Varlamova and Yurina 2006 (10.1134/s0036024406080164), ternary LiClO4-DEC-PC at 25 C by the method of sections: abstract read (attempt 1, row TK-076: no number, solid phase is pure LiClO4, no solvate); fulltext paywalled, no open location (attempt 2); publisher page, 3 KB without abstract or text (attempt 3). Missing: the saturation composition on the PC-rich edge, in mass fraction, mole fraction or molality.
  - Kirillov et al. 2014 (10.1016/j.molliq.2014.08.008), binary LiClO4-PC phase diagram (reported as eutectic type): no abstract from any route (attempt 1); fulltext paywalled (attempt 2); ScienceDirect page HTTP 403 (attempt 3). Missing: the liquidus (solubility against temperature) and its composition scale.
  - Barthel et al. 1993 (10.1016/0022-0728(93)80059-q), conductivity and density of LiClO4 in PC + acetonitrile from infinite dilution to saturation, -35 to 35 C: fulltext paywalled (attempt 1); the Regensburg repository page did not connect, status 000 (attempt 2). Missing: the saturation concentration at 25 C, and the density of the saturated solution that would convert molal to molar.
  - Muhuri et al. 1993 (10.1021/je00010a014), solubilities of alkali-metal salts in PC at 25 C: the abstract route returned the journal page header with no numbers (attempt 1); fulltext paywalled (attempt 2). Whether LiClO4 is among the salts is not known from the text read.
  - Rabadanov et al. 2024 (10.6060/ivkkt.20246705.6967): no retrievable copy (attempt 1, no candidate location).
  - Read in full and not a solubility: Chernozhuk and Kalugin 2016 (10.23939/chcht10.01.009), conductivity in 1e-4 to 2e-2 mol dm^-3 only, LiClO4 non-associated (the first URL, a test host, failed on DNS; the second retrieved the PDF). Gong et al. 2015 (10.1039/c5ee02341f): about 0.7 M is the conductivity maximum, stated for the case that solubility allows it (row TK-081); it is not a saturation concentration.
  - Proxy only: KClO4 in PC, (4.57 +- 0.20) x 10^-2 mol kg^-1 at 25 C, molal (row TK-080, Peruzzi et al. 2015, 10.1007/s10953-015-0335-z). A different cation; it bounds nothing for LiClO4.
  The scale of a future source decides the key: a molal value is stored as is (the key's unit is mol kg^-1); a molar value needs the printed density of the saturated solution (params_README section 9.2 applies the same rule to binding constants).
- **Mean ionic activity coefficient of LiClO4 in PC at 1 mol dm^-3 (measured or Debye-Hueckel): gap.** Searched the same routes with "activity coefficient", "osmotic coefficient", "Debye-Huckel", "Pitzer" and "vapor pressure". Closest open treatments, none for LiClO4 in PC: Self et al. 2021 (10.1149/1945-7111/ac3e47) computes gamma+- with Debye-Hueckel, Born solvation and ion pairing for LiPF6 in PC and LiClO4 in DMC, up to 1 molal, molal scale, as a plot offset by an arbitrary constant with no printed values; Li+ and PF6- radii in PC are rows TK-077 and TK-078 and the activity plot is row TK-079, all comparators. The same paper states Debye-Hueckel alone is insufficient above about 0.01 m, so a Debye-Hueckel extrapolation to 1 mol dm^-3 would not be a measured or accepted value. Xin et al. 2018 (10.1016/j.jct.2018.12.016) prints osmotic and activity coefficients at 25 C for LiCl, LiBr, LiNO3, LiPF6 and LiTFSI in DMC, DMSO and acetonitrile: no PC and no LiClO4. Missing: printed gamma+- values (or osmotic coefficients, or cell-potential data) for LiClO4 in PC, or a Debye-Hueckel treatment printing the ion-size parameter for that salt and solvent.
- **Saturated mean ionic activity of LiClO4 in PC: gap.** No source gives it. It needs the saturation concentration and gamma+- at saturation on one concentration scale; both are missing (keys liclo4_solubility_pc and liclo4_gamma_pc are registered as gaps), and a molar saturation value would also need the density of the saturated solution.
