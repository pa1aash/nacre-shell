## PH-W1.3 (day 5): rows TT-054 to TT-091

Searches ran on openalex, crossref, europepmc and semanticscholar; each source had at most three fetch attempts (all logged in `instrument_gaps.csv`).

- **Hydration Gibbs energies:** Ca2+ already had six rows; Al3+ now has TT-054 (Marcus 1991 value read in a full-text paper, grade C) and TT-055 (rescaled to the cluster-pair proton scale); Li+ has the conventional values of the Tissandier and Fawcett scales (TT-056, TT-057) beside TK-001. The standard-state shift between the two conventions is TT-058 (Marcus 1987). **ClO4-: gap.** Marcus 1991 (10.1039/ft9918702995, 109 ions) is paywalled and its abstract has no numbers; the Madrid-2019 perchlorate paper (10.1016/j.molliq.2025.128035) has no retrievable copy; Housecroft 2017 (10.1039/c6ra25804b) tabulates enthalpies; Tissandier 1998 and the Zheng-Green dataset (organic and singly charged ions) have none.
- **Water to carbonate-solvent transfer:** ClO4- to propylene carbonate, one determination of twelve (TT-059, read from the page image). **Ca2+ and Al3+: gap** (not listed for propylene carbonate in Marcus 1983 or Gritzner 1988; no source for ethylene or dimethyl carbonate). Li+ in propylene carbonate is TI-077.
- **Ca(PO3)2:** crystal DfH and S for the alpha, beta and gamma polymorphs from a CALPHAD assessment (TT-060 to TT-065, Ding 2015; the values are those of Hudon and Jung, whose paper was not read). With the NBS glass DfH (TR-032) the glass-minus-crystal difference is about +19 kJ/mol. **Glass entropy: gap. Calorimetric crystallisation enthalpy: gap** (no heat of crystallisation of a Ca polyphosphate glass was found; Sandstrom 2006 is paywalled).
- **Al(PO3)3:** **DfH, DfG, S and any AlPO4 + P2O5 reaction data: gap.** The only record is a digest abstract that the Ca(PO3)2-Al(PO3)3 system is a simple eutectic (TT-091, original 1974, paywalled). No glass-to-crystal pair for Al or Ca polyphosphate; the Na pair (TT-049) and Li pair remain the analogs.
- **Polyphosphate hydrolysis, pH 7 to 13:** **numeric rate constants: gap.** Abstract-only records of the classical work (TT-079 to TT-082: pyrophosphate and triphosphate at 65.5 C, tetrametaphosphate minimum near pH 7.5, cation and NaOH effects) and one bound, below 10 % in 30 min at pH 7.0 (TT-078).
- **Conversion temperatures:** found, with a method spread (TT-066 to TT-077): brushite to monetite from 160 C (XRD) or 110 to 215 C (TG), monetite to pyrophosphate from 375 C (XRD) to 436 C (TG), gamma to beta pyrophosphate at 535 C. The first XRD source is a preprint; the older values are secondary citations (grade D).
- **Coacervate interfacial tension:** complex coacervates of synthetic polyelectrolytes, 180 to 880 uN/m at pH 6.5, 100 mM NaCl, 25 C (TT-083 to TT-087), with abstract-only values of 204 uN/m, about 100 uN/m and below 1 mN/m (TT-088 to TT-090). **Polyphosphate-containing coacervate: gap** (no measurement found; Spruijt 2010, Priftis 2012 and Ali 2019 are paywalled or without a retrievable copy).
- **Al-polyphosphate complexation at pH 5 to 13 and Ksp of Al polyphosphate or aluminium metaphosphate: gap** (five query formulations, no result).
