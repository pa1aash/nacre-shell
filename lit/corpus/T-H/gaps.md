# T-H gaps

- **Ca2+-template binding paired with gamma_eff_het on the same surface (item 1).** Searched OpenAlex and Crossref for template-directed CaCO3 nucleation against binding free energies (SAMs, polysaccharides, polyelectrolytes, peptoids, simulation of COOH monolayers) and followed the reference lists of Hamm 2014, Nielsen 2020 and Knight 2024. Every numeric pair found measures crystal-template binding (calcite {104} against a SAM-coated tip by dynamic force spectroscopy: Hamm 2014, Nielsen 2020), not Ca2+-template binding. The only Ca2+-template binding free energies found (MUA monolayer, TH-033 to TH-035) come from a work with no nucleation data. Pairing them with Hu 2012 MUA gamma_eff (TC-058) mixes two groups and conditions, so no LFER on Ca2+ binding is recorded. No source found reports binding of an ACC cluster or nucleus to a template as a free energy.
- **Giuffre et al. 2013 PNAS (10.1073/pnas.1222162110) full text and SI.** Europe PMC full-text XML (PMC3677451) returned HTTP 500 on three attempts. The PMC and Europe PMC PDF renders, the publisher PDF (redirect loop) and the VTechWorks handle 10919/51821 (HTTP 503) were all tried and none could be retrieved. Only the abstract (TH-025) and the values compiled in Knight 2024 (TH-020, TH-021) were used. The substrate-water interfacial energies Giuffre derived could not be read. Related theses (Giuffre 2015, Knight 2024, VTechWorks) carry no DOI and were not retrieved.

These would give the numeric crystal-monolayer interfacial energies and binding energies behind the D-grade rows.
- **Nielsen et al. 2020 SI Table S5 (COSMO-RS gamma_sl and gamma_cs for COOH, NH2 and 1:1 SAMs).** Tried the publisher SI URL (not retrieved), the figshare/ChemRxiv preprint (10.26434/chemrxiv.11365025.v1; the cached copy is the article without SI) and the KU research-profile record (no PDF). Still unreachable. With it, gamma_organic_water and gamma_sub_nuc for COOH SAMs would be first-principles numbers.
- **gamma_organic_water for COOH SAMs, catechol small-molecule surfaces, chitosan and alginate.** Searched OpenAlex for COOH-SAM, chitosan and alginate surface free energy components (the route was rate-limited, HTTP 429, after three queries; Crossref was used afterwards). For chitosan, Terpilowski 2022 gives an apparent total and the electron-donor component (TH-058, TH-059) but no water surface tension, so gamma_SL was not derived. In Persin 2023 (10.3390/jfb14040232) the chitosan values sit in figures and the water angle of 117.7 deg on the chitosan film is anomalous, so no row was made. Cichomski 2020 (10.3390/ma13225137) covers fluorinated acids, not COOH-terminated surfaces. The only gamma_organic_water values are derived for PDA (TH-049, TH-052, TH-054, TH-056). They disagree in sign (-27 vs +1.5 to +3.4 mJ/m2), which reflects the model used (van Oss three-liquid vs Owens-Wendt two-liquid). Both treat PDA as a strongly water-attracting surface.
- **Separately determined gamma_sub_nuc or measured contact angle of CaCO3 on an organic surface (item 3).** Searched for GISAXS, AFM and TEM cap profiles of CaCO3 on organic films and for simulations of calcite or ACC on COOH or catechol surfaces. Found only theory statements (TH-036, TH-037), a face-to-face spread compiled from earlier simulations (TH-038), an abstract-level mis-ranking (TH-042) and a mineral-substrate inversion that rests on assumed angles (TH-048). Hu 2012 SI explains that only alpha_cf - alpha_fs is computable but prints no value. No measured contact angle on any organic surface was found. No numeric delta_gamma_water_displacement was found; it is evidenced only qualitatively (TH-024).
- **gamma_oxide_water as a free energy (item 4).** Searched Crossref and web discovery for alpha-Al2O3, gibbsite and boehmite water interfacial free energies (simulation, nanoparticle solubility, wicking, spreading pressure). Wang et al. 2022 gives complete wetting and a bound only (TH-060, TH-061). Holysz and Chibowski 1992 (10.1021/la00038a067, alpha-alumina wicking components) and the DFT boehmite aqueous surface energies (10.1021/acs.jpcc.8b00278) are paywalled. Dubois and Belin-Ferre (10.1186/s40563-015-0046-0) give alumina LW/AB data only in figures. Air-exposed anodic oxides (TH-062 to TH-065) show water angles of 57-91 deg, not the 0 deg of a clean hydroxylated surface, so no gamma_SL was derived from them.
- **gamma_oxide_polymer (oxide-PDA or oxide-catechol polymer interfacial energy in contact) (item 5).** No source found in the searches above. Can only be built from the dry component rows (TH-050, TH-051, TH-053, TH-062 to TH-065) through a combining rule, which this thread did not do.
