## A. One-page summary

**What the paper does.** It screens barrier films for aluminium current collectors in fluorine-free lithium-ion cells, using tabulated thermochemistry with propagated uncertainties. The film chemistries are drawn from the biomineral lineage (carbonate and calcium phosphate, with an optional aluminophosphate route layer). The output is a regime map and a design rule.

**The regime map and the design rule.** The map sorts film chemistries by the conditions the aluminium meets: aqueous alkaline electrode processing, and anodic polarisation in a fluorine-free electrolyte. The design rule says to choose the barrier chemistry that matches the regime, not one chemistry for all of them. The exact wording and the regime boundaries are in the paper (main text, results and discussion).

**What the thermodynamic screen excludes.** It thermodynamically excludes film and electrolyte combinations whose dissolution or conversion reaction is favoured under the stated conditions, within the propagated uncertainty. An exclusion is a statement about equilibrium, not about rates.

**What stays open.** Kinetics, film microstructure, adhesion, coverage and defects, ageing, and the behaviour of real electrodes and cells are not addressed by the screen. Any ranking of surviving chemistries is a ranking on thermodynamic grounds only.

**The narrow suitability statement.** Where the paper calls a film thermodynamically suitable, it does so only with its stated conditions (phase, pH and potential window, ion activities, temperature). It is a necessary condition for dissolution stability only. It is not evidence of protection, of performance, or of cell lifetime.

**Nucleation and templating results are hypotheses.** The nucleation and templating statements were checked by a retrodiction against literature outcomes. It scored below the pass threshold fixed in advance (score and threshold are in the paper). They are therefore presented as hypotheses to be tested, and nothing in the paper depends on them.

**The conditional experiment (Experiment 1).** Conditional on a lab being available (see `braun_note.md` and `experiment_1.md`): aluminium foil of six kinds (bare, carbon-coated, carbonate, calcium phosphate, aluminophosphate, and a route-layer film), (i) immersed in a model alkaline slurry, and (ii) held anodically at 3.65 V and at 4.3 to 4.5 V in a fluorine-free electrolyte. It would test the thermodynamic screen's exclusions directly. The paper does not report it and does not assume its outcome.
