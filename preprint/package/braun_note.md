Subject: A thermodynamic screening paper on aluminium current collectors, and one request

Dear Prof. Braun,

The paper we are preparing for ChemRxiv screens nacre- and bone-inspired barrier films for aluminium current collectors in fluorine-free lithium-ion cells, using tabulated thermochemistry with propagated uncertainties. It gives a regime map, that is, which candidate phases are thermodynamically allowed in the aqueous electrode slurry (pH 10 to 13) and which in the charged cell at 3.65 V and at 4.3 to 4.5 V, and from it a regime-matched design rule. The screen excludes the phases that fail the condition of their regime. What stays open is everything beyond thermodynamics: whether a film forms on foil, how well it adheres and covers, and how it behaves over time. The narrow suitability statement the paper makes is a necessary condition only: a phase the screen excludes is not expected to be suitable; a phase it allows is not shown to be.

The experiment that would test the screen is in the attached text (preprint/package/experiment_1.md). In short, aluminium foil of six kinds (bare, carbon-coated, carbonate, calcium phosphate, aluminophosphate, and a polyphosphate film from our route) is (a) immersed in a model alkaline slurry at pH 10 to 13, and (b) held at constant anodic potential at 3.65 V and at 4.3 to 4.5 V in a fluorine-free electrolyte. We make no claim about any film; the point is to see where the screen is contradicted.

One question: could your group run any part of it, for example only the immersion test, or only the bare and carbon-coated references?

Thank you for considering it.

Palaash Gang
on behalf of the authors
