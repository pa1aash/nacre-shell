# Experiment 1: package text (PH-W2.4b, working text, conditional)

Reused by the co-author notice and by the Predictions section. Wording is neutral and conditional: it states what is proposed to be measured, and which outcomes would count for or against the screen. It reports no result and claims no performance.

## What is proposed

Aluminium foil carrying one of six surface conditions is exposed to two tests, so that the screen's two regimes (aqueous electrode processing and the charged cell) are each checked on the same foil types.

**Foil conditions**

| Label | Condition |
|---|---|
| (i) | bare foil (reference) |
| (ii) | carbon-coated foil (reference) |
| (iii) | carbonate film: calcite or aragonite, whichever the screen selects for the test regime |
| (iv) | calcium phosphate film |
| (v) | aluminophosphate film |
| (vi) | route-layer polyphosphate film (the in situ, matrix-mediated route of the redirect plan) |

**Test (a): immersion in a model alkaline slurry.** Foil of each condition is immersed in a model aqueous electrode slurry at pH 10 to 13, the pH range of the screen's slurry regime (primary range of the redirect plan). Readouts proposed: visible gas evolution or pitting, mass change, and film retention by surface inspection. The slurry is a model (water, base, and the dissolved calcium and phosphate dose of the screen's design axes), not a full electrode formulation.

**Test (b): anodic potential holds in a fluorine-free electrolyte.** Foil of each condition is held at constant anodic potential in a cell with a fluorine-free electrolyte, at two holds: 3.65 V (the LFP-class cutoff) and 4.3 to 4.5 V (the upper cutoff class of the screen), potentials stated against Li/Li+. Readout proposed: the current as a function of time, followed by surface inspection after the hold. Temperature and hold time are to be agreed with whoever runs the test; a higher temperature is the harsher condition.

## How to read the outcomes (conditional)

- The screen states a necessary condition: a film that the screen excludes is not expected to hold under the matching test. A film that survives a test would show that the necessary condition is not contradicted for that film; it would not show that the film is suitable, because suitability needs more than thermodynamic stability (kinetics, coverage, adhesion).
- A film that fails a test its regime says it should pass would count against the screen in that regime and would be reported as such.
- A single bare-foil or carbon-coated result sets the reference for the other conditions; no comparison is made without it.

## What the experiment does not do

It does not test cycling, capacity, impedance or full cells. It does not measure thermodynamic quantities. It does not establish that any film is protective.

## Dependencies stated plainly

The polyphosphate film (vi) is a hypothesis of the route layer. Its formation on foil is itself part of what is open. If the group cannot make it, conditions (i) to (v) can run without it.
