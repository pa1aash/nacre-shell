\section{Templating detail}\label{si:templating}
% The derivation steps behind the Templating thermodynamics and the tier, stage and route detail of the Design rules.
% PH-W8.13: the sentences that repeated the main text verbatim were removed; what is kept adds a step, a bound or a
% definition the main text cites here. One sentence per line with its ledger id.
\paragraph*{Adhesion in water.}
In the slurry the template never meets a dry oxide: the hydroxylated \ce{Al2O3}/\ce{AlOOH} surface carries hydrogen-bonded water, and the template is hydrated. % CL-R01
The reversible work per area that separates the contact into two wetted surfaces is the Dupr\'e work in water, % CL-R01
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dupre}
\Wwet=\gow+\gpw-\gop=\Wdry+2\gw-W_\mathrm{ow}-W_\mathrm{pw},
\end{equation}
with $W_\mathrm{ow}$ and $W_\mathrm{pw}$ the works of water binding and $\Wdry=\go+\gp-\gop$ the adhesion against vacuum. % CL-R01
A large dry adhesion is offset by the binding of water to each surface, so $\Wwet$ can be small or negative where $\Wdry$ is large. % CL-R01
The dry work describes a state the slurry never presents and is kept only as a comparator. % CL-R01
$\Wwet$ is the potential of mean force of a template fragment pulled from the oxide in explicit water, per contact area, and includes the entropy of the released water. % CL-R01, CL-D03
\paragraph*{Binding and the Gibbs adsorption step.}
The transfer free energy $\DGseq$ is built as a thermodynamic cycle that includes the deprotonation of the site at the slurry pH and the activity corrections at its ionic strength. % CL-R02
At equilibrium binding lowers the local standard potential of the ion by exactly as much as it raises the concentration term, and a mean electrostatic potential cancels in the neutral solid. % CL-R02
Bound \ce{Ca^2+} is a surface excess $\GCa=\Gs\Theta-\ds\cb$, with $\Theta$ the Langmuir occupancy of sites of areal density $\Gs$ in a layer of thickness $\ds$, and $\cb$ the free-ion concentration. % CL-R02
The Gibbs adsorption equation turns that excess into a change of the template--liquid energy, % CL-R02
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gibbs}
\dga=\gsl-\gslz=-\Rg T\big[\Gs\ln(1+x)-\ds\cb\big],\qquad x=\frac{\ds\cb}{\Gs}\,\e^{-\DGseq/\Rg T},
\end{equation}
which is negative whenever the excess $\GCa$ is positive; $\gslz$ is the energy of the \ce{Ca}-free template. % CL-R02
The neutral-solute treatment leaves out the change of the diffuse-layer free energy when binding changes the surface charge. % CL-R02
Ionic strength screens that correction, and the model assigns it no sign. % CL-R02
\paragraph*{Young's relation and single entry.}
Young's relation carries the bare $\gnl$, the same energy that sets the homogeneous barrier. % CL-R03
Water displaced from under the contact sits on the contact disc and never touches the free surface of the cap. % CL-R03, CL-R05
A displacement cost therefore enters $\gsn$ once, as a positive correction that raises $\theta$, and not at all when $\gsn$ is already a full in-water contact energy. % CL-R03, CL-R05
Crediting the bound ions to the driving force would give binding the opposite effect on the barrier, and crediting them to both would count the binding free energy twice (Section~\ref{thsi:sec:nodouble}). % CL-R05
\paragraph*{Nucleus density and the wetting bound.}
If nuclei land independently, the number under one contact patch is Poisson distributed, and allowing at most a fraction $q_0$ of patches to stay empty gives $N_\mathrm{req}$. % CL-R11
The count ignores exclusion zones around growing islands, so it is an upper estimate and the criterion is necessary, not sufficient. % CL-R11
The bound holds because $\gsn\ge0$: a negative interfacial free energy between two immiscible solids would make them create contact without limit. % CL-R12
Hydrophilic is meant here in that relative sense: a small water contact angle in air does not by itself place $\gsl$ below $\gnl$. % CL-R12
Whether a template meets the condition is a question for the inputs, and the screen reports the prior mass with $\gsl>\gnl$. % CL-R12
Classical nucleation theory is used with its capillarity approximation and an isotropic $\gnl$, without prenucleation clusters or liquid precursors other than the coacervate of the route layer. % CL-L06
Also left out are the double-layer term of the adsorption energy, kinetics beyond the accessibility bound, any local excess of driving force during drying, and the persistence of the layer in the cell. % CL-L06
\paragraph*{Tiers, stages and routes.}
Tier two is a measured service limit $E_\mathrm{svc}$ from a potential hold on coated foil, a coated cathode particle cycled to a stated cutoff, or voltammetry of the bulk phase, in that order of weight. % CL-D09
A hold or particle record above the cutoff makes a tier-one fall read kinetically stabilised (class A or B), with the tier-one exclusion printed beside it. % CL-D09
A record in a fluorinated electrolyte, where aluminium fluoride forms on the foil, can counter-indicate but never support. % CL-D09
The lithium carbonate record that words the low-voltage claim has a contrary reading. % CL-S15
With protic impurities in a carbonate electrolyte, carbon dioxide evolves from lithium carbonate from about \NumTierTwoContrary\ (grade D, abstract only)\cite{freiberg2020li2co3}. % CL-S15
Slurry-stage phases are brushite, amorphous calcium phosphate and hydroxyapatite; cell-stage phases are calcium pyrophosphate and metaphosphate and the aluminium phosphates. % CL-D11
A conversion step joins a precursor to its cell-stage product through the water activity at which it dehydrates or condenses, the literature conversion temperature and the cure range of the baked aluminophosphate. % CL-D11
The fluorine-free analogue of aluminium fluoride would be an aluminium phosphate or borate film. % CL-D12
The screen tests its oxidation onset, its insolubility in the electrolyte and its blocking of the corrosion ions; the supply of phosphate to a pit is a hypothesis outside the screen. % CL-D12
Calcium polyphosphate trapped by fast drying has no formation criterion of its own and is decided by hydrolysis and the amorphous onset alone. % CL-D13
The wet time $t_\mathrm{wet}$ of the hydrolysis descriptor runs from mixing to the end of drying. % CL-D14
Complexation by polyphosphate lowers the free ligand, not the free cation, so with the oxide film present the free \ce{Al^3+} activity is pinned by the oxide. % CL-D15
Adsorption of polyphosphate on alumina lowers both energies equally, because the coexisting liquids have equal chemical potentials of every component, so it gives the oxide no preference. % CL-D16

\section{Methods detail and the carbonate control}\label{si:methods}
% carbonate control (frozen)
% The prior rule, the propagation and sizing rule, the decision rule with its sweeps, the retrodiction rule, the
% Pourbaix settings with the data modifications the licence asks to be stated, and the carbonate control with its
% transparency notes. PH-W8.13 removed the carbonate-era link table (nothing cited it) and the compute-anchor
% paragraph (one sentence kept). One sentence per line, each with its ledger id; every number is a macro.
% (1) Priors.
\paragraph*{Priors.}
Every input of the chain is a prior built from fetched evidence by one rule, fixed before any value entered the parameter table and before any screen output existed. % CL-M01
Clauses for truncation, widening, analog, ignorance, derived and recorded entries were added later, after some values existed; they select or move no value by judgement. % CL-M01
A literature row is admitted only if it measures the quantity the input defines, under its conditions or under conditions stated as different. % CL-M01
A proxy, a different quantity used in its place, is never admitted, and rows that cite one original determination count once. % CL-M01
Grades run from A and B, for determinations read at source, to C for model fits, derivations and compilations, and D for abstract-only or unchecked secondary citations. % CL-M01
A normal prior needs concordant grade-A or grade-B values with stated uncertainties; grade-C and grade-D rows give only uniform or log-uniform priors over the sourced span. % CL-M01
Grade-D rows enter only where nothing better exists. % CL-M01
Inputs that a source or the physics ties together form correlation groups, held independent where no source reports a correlation, so that the sensitivity to that assumption can be tested. % CL-M01
A binding free energy that rests on a single source is widened to at least the method error of its class and flagged. % CL-M01
Unmeasured interfacial energies carry ignorance priors, uniform between zero, which a sharp interface requires, and the sum of the two dry surface energies. % CL-M02
Where measured analogs exist, the analog prior runs beside the ignorance prior, flagged, and its verdict is stated as conditional on the analog. % CL-M02

% (2) Propagation and sensitivity.
\paragraph*{Propagation and sensitivity.}
For every state of each regime, Monte Carlo draws from the joint prior are propagated through the relations of the main text, with Gaussian-copula rank correlations. % CL-M03
Sobol indices carry bootstrap confidence intervals; Shapley effects share variance among correlated inputs and are repeated under independence as a check. % CL-M03
Draws are raised until every verdict-band mass has a half-width of at most \NumSizingBandHalfWidth\ and the leading total-order Sobol indices one of at most \NumSizingSobolHalfWidth. % CL-M03
A half-width is twice the Monte Carlo standard error. % CL-M03
A run stops at \NumSizingDrawCap\ base draws per regime state or \NumSizingWallClockCap\ of wall-clock time, and a capped run reports the half-widths it reached. % CL-M03
Draws stream in chunks of \NumSizingChunk, so memory does not grow with the draw count. % CL-M03
The required precision of a governing input is the least narrowing of its prior about its median, with every other input at its prior, that decides the verdict. % CL-M03
It is reported as a width in the variable of that prior, or as unreachable when no narrowing of that input alone decides the verdict. % CL-M03

% (3) The decision rule and its sweeps.
\paragraph*{Decision rule.}
The statistic is the prior-weighted mass on each side of an edge. % CL-M04
The primary credible level is $p_\mathrm{cred}=\NumPcred$, with sweeps at \NumPcredSweepLow\ and \NumPcredSweepHigh; the nucleation margin $m_{\cos}=\NumMcos$ has sweeps at \NumMcosSweepLow\ and \NumMcosSweepHigh. % CL-M04
A verdict that changes within a sweep is reported as sensitive to that convention. % CL-M04
The admissible fraction of empty patches is $q_0=1-p_\mathrm{cred}$ and the degradation step is $p_\mathrm{deg}=1-p_\mathrm{cred}$, so both move with the sweep. % CL-M04
A condition with several tests is good on a draw only if every test is, and coverage if either route is; its masses are formed on the same draws. % CL-M04
With a state missing, a worst-state condition can still be ruled out at an evaluated state but cannot pass. % CL-M04
For the accessibility path sum a pass stands and a shortfall is inconclusive, because the missing terms can only add nuclei. % CL-M04
In the carbonate chain the deletion test replaces the template by the bare oxide and by poly(vinylidene fluoride), and a pass must degrade or it describes generic adhesion. % CL-M05
Each framing runs as a whole chain with its own solubility product, molar volume and nucleus--liquid energy, and the chain is ruled out only if it is ruled out under every framing. % CL-M05

% carbonate control (frozen)
% (4) Retrodiction.
\paragraph*{Retrodiction rule.}
The nucleation link is tested against literature cases with measured outcomes. % CL-T02
The case selection, the cases, the correctness rule and the pass rule were fixed before the run; the controls and the interface mapping were added in a later revision, also before the run. % CL-T02
The usable cases are pairwise orderings within one study, and each input maps to the interface it belongs to. % CL-T02
\ce{Ca^2+} binding maps to the template--liquid energy through the Gibbs--Langmuir term, and template--crystal adhesion to the template--nucleus energy. % CL-T02
The one case initially specified through a local enrichment of the driving force is remapped to that bookkeeping. % CL-T02
Each case propagates \NumRetroDraws\ joint draws, with the template-independent inputs shared by both surfaces, and scores the per-draw difference of the two barriers. % CL-T02
A case is correct when the central \NumRetroIntervalLevel\ interval of that difference excludes zero on the observed side, and incorrect when it excludes zero on the other side. % CL-T02
Otherwise it is inconclusive, and an inconclusive case counts as a miss. % CL-T02
The retrodiction passes if at least \NumRetroPassCount\ of the \NumRetroUsableCases\ usable cases are correct, the smallest count with a probability of at most \NumRetroChanceBound\ under independent fair coins. % CL-T02
The barrier code never sees the observed relation, and swapping test and reference must reverse the sign of the difference draw by draw. % CL-T02
A permutation null over the case labels is a descriptive control. % CL-T02
Several cases share a study or a reference surface, and all observed relations run the same way. % CL-T02
The source already reports the input--outcome link in \NumRetroLinkReported\ of the \NumRetroUsableCases\ cases, no hydroxyl-terminated case could be built, and only the sign of a within-study difference is tested. % CL-T02

% (5) Pourbaix and window settings.
\paragraph*{Pourbaix and window settings.}
Solid-phase energies come from the Materials Project\cite{jain2013commentary}, database version \NumMpDatabaseVersion, retrieved through its interface. % CL-M06
They are used under CC~BY~\NumMpLicenceVersion\ (\url{https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/}); entries derived from GNoME, licensed CC~BY-NC~\NumMpLicenceVersion, were left out, and every entry used carries the CC~BY tag. % CL-M06
We modified the data in four ways. % CL-M06
The \NumMpCorrectionScheme\ compatibility corrections and the aqueous-compatibility adjustments were applied. % CL-M06
The Pourbaix entries were rebuilt offline from the solid entries and the aqueous ion reference data, following Persson and co-workers\cite{persson2012prediction}. % CL-M06
Amorphous calcium carbonate was represented as calcite plus a literature free-energy offset. % CL-M06
For the lithium-referenced window an all-experimental route replaced the computed energies where experimental data exist, and the computed gas-reference schemes are benchmarked comparators. % CL-M06
The aqueous ion reference data are the MPContribs project ``Aqueous Ion Reference Data'', owned by its contributors and licensed CC~BY~\NumMpLicenceVersion. % CL-M06
Use of these data does not imply endorsement by the Materials Project or by the contributors of this work. % CL-M06
The slurry construction holds carbon at the dissolved inorganic carbon of the regime and removes dissolved calcium species from the hull, because dissolution is the $\Omb$ test. % CL-M06
It evaluates the worst point of the pH and potential region against a tolerance from the formation-energy error of the database\cite{stevanovic2012correcting,wang2021framework}. % CL-M06
A construction that allows carbon redox is a labelled sensitivity. % CL-M06
The $\Omb$ test must report dissolution at an undersaturated control state and stability at a supersaturated one, and a failed control makes the verdict inconclusive. % CL-M06
Two gas-reference schemes, with the tabulated and with the database \ce{CO2}, were benchmarked against measured free energies of carbonate decomposition and oxidation. % CL-M06
They bracket experiment from opposite sides. % CL-M06
The all-experimental route uses tabulated thermochemistry for every species\cite{rossini1971selected,rossini1981selected,allison2013nist}. % CL-M06
Its margin $m_\mathrm{expt}$ combines the tabulated uncertainties per electron with a bound on the neglected solid entropy of \ce{Ca(ClO4)2} from analog salts, and it does not hold $m_\mathrm{sink}$. % CL-M06
Where an input of that route is missing, a limit is decided over the computed schemes and marked as such. % CL-M06
A pass then needs the scheme with the lowest onset, and a phase is ruled out only if every scheme rules it out. % CL-M06
A limit whose sink thermochemistry is missing is undetermined and never counts as a pass. % CL-M06
A planned anchor calculation, replacing inner-shell water of hydrated \ce{Ca^2+} by a carboxylate or catecholate ligand, was not run and enters no input or verdict. % CL-M07
