## AI-use disclosure: policy requirement

The track places the disclosure in the Acknowledgements (`ops/tracks/p0.yaml`, `inputs.ai_disclosure`). Its text lives only in the local-only include `.local/p0/ai_ack.tex`, which is never committed.

`ops/policy/p0/requirements.md` (T2, T5 and conflicts 1 and 2) requires the following:

- **The statement must name the tool and the method of use.** ChemRxiv asks for the "name of the tool(s) and method of use" [T1-2]. RSC asks for the tool's name and its specific model or version [T4-1].
- **The tool is never listed as an author,** in the document or in the submission information.
- **The authors remain accountable** for the text.
- **The released PDF will therefore contain the tool name,** although the repository does not.

P0-W8.5 writes the wording with Palaash.

The open ruling for the later RSC journal route is recorded in requirements.md, conflict 7. That route would also need a cover-letter declaration and a Methods statement that includes the prompts.
