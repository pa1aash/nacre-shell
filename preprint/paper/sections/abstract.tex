Aluminium current collectors in water-processed, fluorine-free lithium-ion cathodes meet an alkaline slurry during processing and an oxidising potential in the cell, and this thermodynamic screen tests mineral phases of the nacre and bone lineages against both stages, per cutoff class.
The regime map gives a design rule: calcium carbonate, as calcite, meets the dissolution condition over part of the alkaline processing grid and is not thermodynamically excluded at the lowest cutoff class, and in acid-adjusted slurries it is excluded against dissolution only below pH \NumRegAcidFirstCalcite, while at high voltage only the condensed calcium phosphates, reached by the amorphous-precursor route of bone mineral, are not thermodynamically excluded and the aluminium phosphates, screened beside them as non-biogenic comparators, are undetermined.
At the solid-salt upper bound plus margin, for a \ce{LiClO4}-saturated sink (solid-salt reference state), the oxidation ladder thermodynamically excludes calcium carbonate above about \NumCarbonateCrossover, hydroxyapatite and the tricalcium phosphates on their crystalline framing, with upper bounds at \NumOnsetBoneLow\ to \NumOnsetBoneHigh, at \NumClassMidHigh\ and above, and brushite in part; no phase passes, the lower bound needing unmeasured transfer free energies, and the aluminium phosphates are undetermined.
The one positive statement is narrow: the label reads thermodynamically suitable against dissolution (a necessary condition) for calcite in the processing grid at total calcium of at least \NumRegWindowFullLoCalcite\ (\NumRegSlurryPassFullCalcite\ cells, every state passing) and pH \NumRegSlurryPhLow\ to \NumRegSlurryPhHigh; it is not evidence of protection.
The nucleation half of the model is not validated.
If the screen's model holds, then in slurry immersion and anodic holds in a fluorine-free electrolyte a calcite film does not dissolve inside its passing region and is not thermodynamically excluded at the low hold, hydroxyapatite is thermodynamically excluded for a \ce{LiClO4}-saturated sink (solid-salt reference state) at the high hold, and condensed calcium phosphate films are not thermodynamically excluded there, while aluminium phosphate films are undetermined.%
