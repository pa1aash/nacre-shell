\section{Discussion and limits}\label{sec:discussion}

% (1) What the screen shows and what it does not.
The screen gives one positive statement and a map of thermodynamic exclusions. % CL-S13
The positive statement is the dissolution label of calcite at the alkaline processing stage, over the passing cells named in Section~\ref{sec:res-dissolution}; it is a necessary condition only, and the screen gives no evidence that any film forms on the foil, that a film keeps the slurry off the metal, or that layers build up. % CL-S17, CL-D20
On the oxidation side, carbonate (crystalline framing) and the calcium orthophosphates (crystalline framing, and amorphous framing by the bound $\Delta G_{\mathrm{g}\to\mathrm{c}}\ge0$ against the crystalline reference plus water) are thermodynamically excluded\nacreExcludedMark\nacreSinkMark\ at the higher cutoff classes, and the condensed calcium phosphates are left not thermodynamically excluded and the aluminium phosphates undetermined, which is where the screen leaves the question open. % CL-S13, CL-S14
% CL-N04, CL-N05, CL-N10, CL-N11, CL-N12, CL-N13: verbatim does-not-claim sentences.
This paper does not claim that any interface, template or film thickness is optimal.
This paper does not claim experimental validation.
This paper does not claim that any film forms on aluminium foil or that a coacervate wets aluminium; formation and wetting are criteria screened thermodynamically.
This paper does not claim that a thermodynamic onset is a measured service limit, or that a record in a fluorinated electrolyte supports a pass.
This paper does not claim any result for a fluorinated electrolyte or any fluoride phase, including as a comparator.
This paper does not claim the acid-adjusted slurry as a regime of the barrier; it is a comparator to the incumbent.

% (2) The limits of the screen, in the order in which they bear on the verdicts.
The largest limit is the sink bracket: $E_\mathrm{lb}$, computed from aqueous formation data, bounds the onset only if $\Delta G_\mathrm{tr}^\mathrm{rel}\ge0$, which no measured transfer free energy of \ce{Ca^2+} or \ce{Al^3+} into the cell solvent tests, so no phase passes the oxidation condition and every open row is open on its upper bound alone. % CL-L11
The upper end assumes the saturated sink\nacreSinkMark: the one-sided correction for an electrolyte below saturation, $m_\mathrm{sink}$, is registered but unevaluated, so an exclusion whose crossover lies near a class edge is stated at the next edge. % CL-L11
Classical nucleation theory with a spherical-cap nucleus of one registered phase is an approximation at nucleus size, amorphous precursors and non-classical pathways lie outside it, and its retrodiction failed (\NumRetroCorrect\ of \NumRetroUsableCases\ against \NumRetroPassCount), so every templating statement is a model statement. % CL-L03, CL-T03
The wetting machinery treats polyphosphate as a neutral solute, and the electrostatic double-layer term of the charged oxide, which differs between a dense coacervate and its dilute phase, is the leading term it leaves out; its sign is not assumed. % CL-L12
Every verdict is thermodynamic: kinetic stabilisation of a phase at the cutoff and the race of gelation or drying against hydrolysis are outside the screen, and the passivation window is a thermodynamic proxy for the state of the oxide, not a rate. % CL-L12, CL-L01
The screen tests the properties a film would need at each stage, not that a layer formed in the slurry persists through drying and cell assembly, and the insulating-thickness bound is not evaluated for want of a film resistivity. % CL-L12, CL-L04
Solid energies come from two database versions, \NumMpDatabaseVersion\ for the carbonate control and \NumMpDatabaseVersionComparator\ for every comparator column, and decide no row. % CL-L13
Several inputs are grade D, among them the published passivation window that the acid-adjusted grid spans (the sourced acid-adjusted range is graded B) and the solubility product of $\alpha$-TCP, and the solubility product of aragonite comes from a compilation of a primary measurement that was not reached, a tertiary source graded C. % CL-L13
Six interpretive choices were made with values in view: the early calcite estimate, the regime framing after the oxidation upper bounds were seen, amorphous aluminium hydroxide as the film solid of the passivation window, the bounds of the glass correction of calcium metaphosphate, the move of the supersaturated control of the dissolution test, and the mapping of a falsified verdict onto the word excluded. % CL-L13, CL-M08
The priors are broad because the literature is, and every verdict is conditional on them; the data that would move them most are the calcium and aluminium transfer free energies into the cell solvent, glass and amorphous thermochemistry of \ce{Ca2P2O7} and amorphous calcium phosphate, $\gsn$, the Gibbs energy or entropy of solid \ce{Al(ClO4)3}, tier-two records in a fluorine-free electrolyte, and the rate of the hydrolysis race. % CL-L02

% (3) Contrary evidence.
The contrary records bear on the slurry stage: calcite scale on aluminium alloys was porous with attack beneath it\cite{osoriocelestino2020influence}, so dissolution stability is not evidence of protection, and a polydopamine coating reduced carbonate deposition on steel\cite{yu2023study}, against a catechol template; neither tests a templated film, and both are weighed, not set aside. % CL-L05, CL-E01, CL-E02
Imide salts sold as fluorine-free alternatives decompose with fluoride as the main product at the aluminium collector\cite{kramer2012mechanism}, so the cell stage here is limited to a salt without fluorine. % CL-L05
In a phosphoric-acid slurry, lithium and aluminium phosphates compete with the calcium phases for the phosphate; \ce{Li3PO4} is undetermined here, and no solubility comparison between them is sourced. % CL-L05
Anodic solvent oxidation makes protons at the aluminium surface\cite{ma2017revisiting}, and structural hydroxide and hydrate water are plausible proton sources near the carbonate limit, which bears on the hydroxyapatite and amorphous rows. % CL-L05
Cyclic voltammetry can overstate a stability window and computed formation energies can understate it\cite{schwietert2021first,binninger2020comparison}, so neither a computed nor a voltammetric tier-two value is a tight bound. % CL-L05

% (4) The incumbents and what is not shown against them.
Against the incumbents the screen shows nothing comparative. % CL-L08
Phosphoric-acid pH control enters as the comparator state of the dissolution test, in which calcite dissolves at every cell only at pH \NumRegAcidExclLoCalcite\ and \NumRegAcidExclHiCalcite, in the lower part of the passivation window and not in the sourced acid-adjusted range, and passes from pH \NumRegAcidFirstCalcite\ up, and the apatites pass at the lowest pH only from \NumRegAcidMinCaHap\ calcium, and the two baselines enter only through their rows; carbon-coated foil appears only as an experimental control. % CL-L08, CL-S08, CL-S11
% CL-N07: verbatim does-not-claim sentence.
This paper does not claim that any film outperforms phosphoric-acid pH control, carbon-coated foil or the baked aluminophosphate baseline.

% (5) Outlook: a computed transfer premise, described and not claimed (cp6 F16).
A computed transfer premise would allow passes labelled (computed premise); none is reported here. % CL-O05
Such a premise would be the transfer free energies of \ce{Li+}, \ce{Ca^2+} and \ce{Al^3+} from water to propylene carbonate in one cluster--continuum method, benchmarked on the alkali ions, and a row would then pass only where its lower bound clears the cutoff, its margin and the uncertainty of the premise; propylene carbonate would stand in for the cyclic carbonates only. % CL-O05

% (6) The experiment as the way forward.
The experiment of Section~\ref{sec:predictions} is the way forward, because it tests the one positive statement, the exclusions and the open rows on the same foils, while a measured relative transfer free energy of calcium, a charge-balanced headroom that decides the low class for the calcium orthophosphates and could admit the calcium metaphosphate glass up to the highest class, or a template--nucleus energy at the precisions of Section~\ref{sec:res-precision} would move the verdicts the experiment leaves open. % CL-Q01, CL-P04
