\section{Discussion and limits}\label{sec:discussion}

% (1) What the screen shows and what it does not.
The screen gives one positive statement and a map of thermodynamic exclusions. % CL-S13
The positive statement is the dissolution label of calcite at the alkaline processing stage, over the passing cells named in Section~\ref{sec:res-dissolution}; it is a necessary condition only, and the screen gives no evidence that any film forms on the foil, that a film keeps the slurry off the metal, or that layers build up. % CL-S17, CL-D20
On the oxidation side, for a \ce{LiClO4}-saturated sink (solid-salt reference state), carbonate and the calcium orthophosphates on their crystalline framing are thermodynamically excluded\nacreExcludedMark\ at the higher cutoff classes, and the condensed and aluminium phosphates are left not excluded or undetermined, which is where bone-inspired chemistry has to be tested. % CL-S13, CL-S14
% CL-N04, CL-N05, CL-N10, CL-N11, CL-N12, CL-N13: verbatim does-not-claim sentences.
This paper does not claim that any interface, template or film thickness is optimal.
This paper does not claim experimental validation.
This paper does not claim that any film forms on aluminium foil or that a coacervate wets aluminium; formation and wetting are criteria screened thermodynamically.
This paper does not claim that a thermodynamic onset is a measured service limit, or that a record in a fluorinated electrolyte supports a pass.
This paper does not claim any result for a fluorinated electrolyte or any fluoride phase, including as a comparator.
This paper does not claim the acid-adjusted slurry as a regime of the barrier; it is a comparator to the incumbent.

% (2) The limits of the screen, in the order in which they bear on the verdicts.
The largest limit is the sink bracket: with no measured transfer free energy of \ce{Ca^2+} or \ce{Al^3+} into the cell solvent its lower end is pending, so no phase passes the oxidation condition and every open row is open on its upper bound alone. % CL-L11
The upper end assumes a \ce{LiClO4}-saturated sink (solid-salt reference state): the one-sided correction for an electrolyte below saturation, $m_\mathrm{sink}$, is registered but unevaluated, so an exclusion whose crossover lies near a class edge is stated at the next edge. % CL-L11
Classical nucleation theory with a spherical-cap nucleus of one registered phase is an approximation at nucleus size, amorphous precursors and non-classical pathways lie outside it, and its retrodiction failed (\NumRetroCorrect\ of \NumRetroUsableCases\ against \NumRetroPassCount), so every templating statement is a model statement. % CL-L03, CL-T03
The wetting machinery treats polyphosphate as a neutral solute, and the electrostatic double-layer term of the charged oxide, which differs between a dense coacervate and its dilute phase, is the leading term it leaves out; its sign is not assumed. % CL-L12
Every verdict is thermodynamic: kinetic stabilisation of a phase at the cutoff and the race of gelation or drying against hydrolysis are outside the screen, and the passivation window is a thermodynamic proxy for the state of the oxide, not a rate. % CL-L12, CL-L01
The screen tests the properties a film would need at each stage, not that a layer formed in the slurry persists through drying and cell assembly, and the insulating-thickness bound is not evaluated for want of a film resistivity. % CL-L12, CL-L04
Solid energies come from two database versions, \NumMpDatabaseVersion\ for the carbonate control and \NumMpDatabaseVersionComparator\ for every comparator column, and decide no row. % CL-L13
Several inputs are grade D, among them the published passivation window that the acid-adjusted grid spans (the sourced acid-adjusted range is graded B) and the solubility product of $\alpha$-TCP, and the solubility product of aragonite comes from a compilation of a primary measurement that was not reached, a tertiary source graded C. % CL-L13
Some interpretive choices were made with values in view: the regime framing after the oxidation upper bounds were seen, amorphous aluminium hydroxide as the film solid of the passivation window, the bounds of the glass correction of calcium metaphosphate, the move of the supersaturated control of the dissolution test, and the mapping of a falsified verdict onto the word excluded. % CL-L13, CL-M08
The priors are broad because the literature is, and every verdict is conditional on them. % CL-L02

% (3) Contrary evidence.
The contrary records point the same way as the exclusions rather than against them: calcite scale on aluminium alloys was porous with attack beneath it\cite{osoriocelestino2020influence}, and a polydopamine coating reduced carbonate deposition\cite{yu2023study}; neither tests a templated film, and both are weighed, not set aside. % CL-L05, CL-E01, CL-E02

% (4) The incumbents and what is not shown against them.
Against the incumbents the screen shows nothing comparative. % CL-L08
Phosphoric-acid pH control enters as the comparator state of the dissolution test, in which calcite dissolves only below pH \NumRegAcidFirstCalcite, in the lower part of the passivation window and not in the sourced acid-adjusted range, and the apatites pass only at high dose at the lowest pH, and carbon-coated foil and the two patent baselines enter only through their rows. % CL-L08, CL-S08, CL-S11
% CL-N07: verbatim does-not-claim sentence.
This paper does not claim that any film outperforms phosphoric-acid pH control, carbon-coated foil or the baked aluminophosphate baseline.
Among the open sources scanned, none reports a crosslinked, water-insoluble polyphosphate film formed in situ on aluminium foil in a fluorine-free battery electrode context; the statement holds only with all four qualifiers, and the patent search remains a logged gap. % CL-V08
Whether a ladder of this kind from tabulated thermochemistry exists elsewhere is not established, because the patent literature was not searched. % CL-L08

% (5) The second version, described and not claimed.
A second version would replace the missing transfer data by a computed premise: transfer free energies of \ce{Li+}, \ce{Ca^2+} and \ce{Al^3+} from water to propylene carbonate, computed in one cluster--continuum method after a benchmark on the alkali ions, would let a row whose lower bound clears the cutoff, its margin and the uncertainty of the premise be reported as a pass labelled (computed premise). % CL-O05
That path is described here and not taken: this version contains no such pass, and propylene carbonate would stand in for the cyclic carbonates only. % CL-O05

% (6) The experiment as the way forward.
The experiment of Section~\ref{sec:predictions} is the way forward, because it tests the one positive statement, the exclusions and the open rows on the same foils, while a measured calcium transfer free energy or template--nucleus energy at the precisions of Section~\ref{sec:res-precision} would move the verdicts the experiment leaves open. % CL-Q01, CL-P04
