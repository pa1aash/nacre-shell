\section{Methods}\label{sec:methods}

% (1) Parameters and priors.
\paragraph*{Parameters and priors.}
Every input is a prior built by one rule from fetched sources, each value with its DOI and locator and its grade limiting the prior family (Table~\ref{tab:parameters}; \nacreSIref{si:sourced}{Sourced parameters}); an input with no value is undetermined and never lets a condition pass. % CL-M11, CL-M01
An unmeasured interfacial energy carries an ignorance prior between its thermodynamic bounds, and a probability of feasibility under such a prior is never reported as a finding; the flip value and the precision that would decide the verdict are reported instead. % CL-M02
Process variables and the cutoff are design axes, never averaged; the cutoff classes are a low class at \NumClassLo, with \NumClassSensitivity\ as a sensitivity line, and classes at \NumClassMidLow\ to \NumClassMidHigh\ and \NumClassHiLow\ to \NumClassHiHigh. % CL-M02, CL-D04

% (2) Onset thermochemistry, its uncertainty budget, the DFT comparator and the sink bracket.
\paragraph*{Oxidation onsets.}
Each onset is the minimum over the oxidation reactions of the phase, staged products included, on the experimental route of tabulated formation data, with a margin that bounds the summed species uncertainties and the neglected sink entropy per electron (Table~\ref{tab:thermo}); a glass without its own data is its crystal plus a correction bounded below by zero, with a labelled uncertainty. % CL-M10, CL-M12
Database formation energies are a comparator only, always printed with their measured error against tabulated enthalpies, benchmarked when it is within \NumDftTolerance\ per atom; the errors span \NumDftCaPhosLow\ to \NumDftCaPhosHigh\ per formula unit over the calcium phosphates of the onset ladder, and no database value decides a row (\nacreSIref{si:thermo}{Thermochemistry benchmark and the DFT spread}). % CL-M10
Every tier-one exclusion is stated for a \ce{LiClO4}-saturated sink (solid-salt reference state): the one-sided correction for a dilute electrolyte, $m_\mathrm{sink}$ of Section~\ref{sec:design}, is registered but unevaluated, so no number is given for it. % CL-M17
$E_\mathrm{lb}$, computed from aqueous formation data at the stated product activity, bounds the onset only if $\Delta G_\mathrm{tr}^\mathrm{rel}\ge0$, which no measured datum tests, because the transfer free energies of \ce{Ca^2+} and \ce{Al^3+} into the cell solvent are unmeasured, so no row can pass and every row rests on the solid-salt bound, which can falsify a phase but cannot pass it. % CL-M17

% (3) Solubility, speciation and the aluminium passivation window.
\paragraph*{Solubility and the aluminium window.}
Supersaturations (Eq.~\eqref{eq:stability}) follow from measured solubility products and the slurry speciation, and the passivation window of aluminium comes from our own Al--O--H Pourbaix construction on benchmarked ion data\cite{persson2012prediction}, governed by amorphous \ce{Al(OH)3}, the least ordered hydroxide and the one expected on the foil in water at room temperature, with boehmite as the reference and both published windows beside them\cite{kuhnel2016water}. % CL-D05, CL-M13

% (3b) Dose grid and control states (PH-W8.2e; cp5b F2 and F4). Ratio-grid run: session PH-W4.6.
\paragraph*{Dose grid and control states.}
The production run of the phosphate dissolution test crossed total calcium and total phosphate independently on one decade grid, \NumRegGridCaCount\ values of each and \NumRegGridPairs\ pairs per cell group, so the Ca:P ratios of its cells are powers of ten and the apatite ratios \NumRatioTcp\ and \NumRatioHap\ do not occur in it. % CL-M04
A ratio grid was run as a separate, labelled run beside the production run: total phosphate is the calcium dose divided by the Ca:P of the phase, \NumRatioHap\ for hydroxyapatite, \NumRatioTcp\ for $\beta$-tricalcium phosphate and \NumRatioBrushite\ for brushite, and amorphous calcium phosphate and the \ce{Ca4H(PO4)3} basis of octacalcium phosphate are run on both apatite ratios, \NumRatioPhases\ phases in all, with \NumRatioCellsAlkaline\ alkaline and \NumRatioCellsAcid\ acid-adjusted cells per phase. % CL-M04
The verdicts are unchanged: \NumRatioVerdictSame\ of \NumRatioVerdictTotal\ phase and grid verdicts agree with the production run, and \NumRatioFlips\ of the \NumRatioGroups\ phase, ratio and pH groups change between passing and failing; \NumRatioGroupsDiffer\ differ in the lowest passing $\mathrm{Ca_T}$ only, by one decade, higher on the ratio line in \NumRatioGroupsHigher\ and lower in \NumRatioGroupsLower. % CL-M04
The Ca:P of the passing cells is reported with each pass count: a range on the crossed grid (hydroxyapatite: \NumRegCaPHapLo\ to \NumRegCaPHapHi; \nacreSIref{si:regime}{Regime table detail and later reruns}) and the ratio itself on a ratio line, where every cell has it. % CL-M04
The supersaturated control state of a phase and state is defined by a rule: the grid state of maximum $\Omega$ for that phase and state, which must report $\Omb\ge1$; the undersaturated control is the low-dose corner of the grid. % CL-M04

% (4) Thresholds and decision rules.
\paragraph*{Thresholds and decision rules.}
Each condition has one decisive quantity and an edge where the physics changes, moved by a margin for method error (Table~\ref{tab:thresholds}); it is feasible if the prior mass on its good side is at least the credible level $p_\mathrm{cred}=\NumPcred$, falsified if the mass on the other side is, and inconclusive otherwise; the nucleation margin is $m_{\cos}=\NumMcos$, and the degradation step of the deletion test is $p_\mathrm{deg}=1-p_\mathrm{cred}$, \NumPdeg\ at the primary level; the published sweeps of both are in \nacreSIref{si:methods}{Methods detail and the carbonate control}. % CL-M04, CL-P02

% (5) Propagation, sensitivity and the required precision.
\paragraph*{Propagation and sensitivity.}
Monte Carlo draws from the joint prior, with copula rank correlations, are propagated through the relations of Section~\ref{sec:design}; Sobol indices rank independent inputs\cite{saltelli2002making,archer1997sensitivity} and Shapley effects correlated ones\cite{iooss2019shapley}, with draws sized by a rule on the half-widths of the band masses (\nacreSIref{si:methods}{Methods detail and the carbonate control}). % CL-M03
The Sobol indices of the nucleation chain reach a total-order half-width of \NumSobolNucHalfWidth\ against the target of \NumSobolNucTarget, capped at \NumSobolNucBase\ base rows, so they are indicative and the Shapley effects carry the ranking. % CL-M03

\paragraph*{Materials Project data.}
Solid-phase energies were taken from the Materials Project\cite{jain2013commentary} (database version \NumMpDatabaseVersion\ for the carbonate control and \NumMpDatabaseVersionComparator\ for every database comparator column), used under the Creative Commons Attribution \NumMpLicenceVersion\ International licence (CC~BY~\NumMpLicenceVersion, \url{https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/}); entries derived from GNoME, licensed CC~BY-NC, were excluded. % CL-M06, CL-M16

% (7) Baselines, the carbonate control and the retrodiction.
\paragraph*{Baselines, control and retrodiction.}
The patent baselines enter as comparator rows through $K_1$, $K_2$ and $K_4$ only, a solubility their patents do not print being undetermined and $K_4$ undetermined for want of a film thickness, and the carbonate control is taken unchanged from the earlier carbonate screen (\nacreSIref{si:methods}{Methods detail and the carbonate control}). % CL-M14, CL-M16
Its nucleation half was tested against literature cases with measured outcomes: \NumRetroCorrect\ of \NumRetroUsableCases\ cases were correct against the \NumRetroPassCount\ the rule required, so the nucleation half is not validated (\nacreSIref{si:retrodiction}{Retrodiction cases}). % CL-T03

% (8) Disclosures.
\paragraph*{Disclosures.}
Six parts of the method were set with some values in view: the early calcite estimate, the regime framing, the film solid of the passivation window, the bounds of the glass correction of calcium metaphosphate, the move of the supersaturated control of the dissolution test, and the mapping of a falsified verdict onto the word excluded. % CL-M08
An earlier rough estimate under the calcite framing was available when the project was designed, and the framings were chosen on literature grounds (\nacreSIref{si:methods}{Methods detail and the carbonate control}). % CL-M08
The upper bounds of the oxidation onsets were seen before the regime-matched frame of this paper was adopted. % CL-M08
Two choices were made after the values they affect had been seen: amorphous \ce{Al(OH)3} as the film solid that governs the passivation window, and the bounds of the glass-to-crystal correction of \ce{Ca(PO3)2}. % CL-M08
The supersaturated control state of the phosphate dissolution test was moved from pH \NumCtlPhFrom\ to pH \NumCtlPhTo\ after a dry run, an output seen before the choice; the new runs use the maximum-$\Omega$ rule, which leaves every cell of the original keys unchanged (\NumCtlCellsMoved\ cells moved). % CL-M08
Calling a falsified verdict excluded was a mapping chosen with the verdicts in view. % CL-M08
The parameter table, the benchmark, the thresholds with their numeric margins and the decision rules were fixed with content hashes before the production screen, and any later change is recorded as a dated deviation with its effect on the verdicts; they were not set before every calculation the project made, as these cases show. % CL-M15, CL-M08
