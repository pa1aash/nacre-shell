\section{Predictions}\label{sec:predictions}

% (1) The initial experiment.
The screen turns into one experiment that tests both stages with the same foils. % CL-Q01
Aluminium foil is tested bare, carbon-coated, and carrying one of four films: calcium carbonate, hydroxyapatite, a baked aluminophosphate, or a film made by one of the in-situ routes. % CL-Q01
Test (a) is immersion in a model alkaline slurry at the pH and calcium doses of the processing grid. % CL-Q01
Test (b) is a set of anodic potential holds at \NumClassLo\ and at \NumHoldLow\ to \NumHoldHigh\ in a fluorine-free electrolyte. % CL-Q01
Lithium perchlorate in a carbonate solvent is one such electrolyte, and its salt is that of the solid-salt sink. % CL-Q01
The observables are the calcium, phosphorus and aluminium released into the liquid, the anodic current against the bare and carbon-coated foils, the gas evolved during the hold, and the film composition before and after. % CL-Q01

% (2) The conditional predictions (cp6 F7, F12): one lead sentence, the refutation rule of K1, and the table.
If the screen's model holds, the experiment has the outcomes of Table~\ref{tab:predictions}, each with its observable and what would refute it. % CL-Q05, CL-Q02, CL-Q03
No hold can refute the $K_1$ predictions: oxidation below an upper bound is allowed, and survival above it is read as kinetic stabilisation, which the screen does not test. % CL-Q03, CL-Q05
Only the dissolution predictions of the immersion can be refuted. % CL-Q05
The bound of \ce{Ca2P2O7}, \NumPyroOnset, lies inside the hold range, and for the aluminium phosphates the screen has no bound at all. % CL-Q03
The hold therefore measures a service limit where the screen has only a ceiling, and where it has none. % CL-Q03

% (3) What would decide the nucleation condition.
Whether any of these films can be grown on a template from the slurry is outside the experiment, and the screen states it only as a model statement. % CL-O04
A measured or computed template--nucleus energy of hydroxyapatite within about \NumNucDecideWithin\ of zero would decide the nucleation condition. % CL-O04
A measured relative transfer free energy of \ce{Ca^2+} against \ce{Li+} into the cell solvent, to about \NumPrecTransferCaKj\ (a charge-balanced headroom), would decide the low class for the calcium orthophosphates. % CL-O04
It could also admit the \ce{Ca(PO3)2} glass up to the highest class; nothing in this paper supplies either measurement. % CL-O04
