\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

% (1) The setting: water-processed cathodes, the alkaline slurry and the native oxide.
Water-processed lithium-ion cathodes replace the fluorinated binder and organic solvent of conventional manufacture with fluorine-free binders cast from water\cite{heidbuchel2022enabling}. % CL-K01
Lithium leaching from the active particles then drives the slurry strongly alkaline\cite{hawley2020lithium,bauer2019effects}, and at that pH the native \ce{Al2O3}/\ce{AlOOH} film on the aluminium collector converts to hydroxide and dissolves, exposing the metal during slurry contact\cite{heidbuchel2022enabling,li2016effect,doberdo2014enabling}. % CL-K02, CL-K03
The slurry stage is therefore a regime of its own, an alkaline aqueous contact between mixing and drying, distinct from the non-aqueous cell that follows, which is a second set of conditions. % CL-K04

% carbonate control (frozen)
% (2) The biomineralisation idea as a physical hypothesis; each link a free-energy question.
Biomineralisation suggests another route. % CL-K05
Nacre grows calcium carbonate on an organic matrix whose macromolecules select the polymorph\cite{falini1996control}; amorphous calcium carbonate appears among the precursor phases at its growth front and coats the mature tablets\cite{devol2015nanoscale,schmidt2024myriad,nassif2005amorphous}, and organic macromolecules can stabilise such a precursor\cite{gong2012phase}. % CL-K05, CL-K06
We treat the transplant of this sequence to the collector as a physical hypothesis with four links, each a free-energy question (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}). % CL-R06
Does a catechol-bearing layer adhere to the hydrated oxide once it has displaced interfacial water? % CL-R06
Does \ce{Ca^2+} bind at the layer, relative to the hydrated ion? % CL-R06
Does the layer template \ce{CaCO3} nucleation, lowering the heterogeneous barrier through its interfacial energies with the nucleus and the solution\cite{giuffre2013polysaccharide}? % CL-R06, CL-R07
Is the resulting film stable in each regime? % CL-R06
How ion binding enters the barrier is made precise in Section~\ref{sec:model}. % CL-R07

% (3) Why the question is thermodynamic, and what is unknown.
The question is thermodynamic: a link that is unfavourable for every plausible input ends the sequence whatever the kinetics. % CL-R08
Those inputs are weakly constrained by experiment. % CL-K07
The oxide--water interfacial free energy is known only through calorimetric surface enthalpies of hydroxylated or dry surfaces\cite{majzlan2000surface,tavakoli2013amorphous}; wet adhesion of catechol-bearing proteins has been measured on mica and silica, not on alumina\cite{lu2013adhesion}; and nucleus--organic interfacial energies exist only as effective values from nucleation-rate fits\cite{giuffre2013polysaccharide,knight2024kinetics}, beside a contact-angle bound on a mineral surface\cite{li2018apparent}. % CL-K07, CL-K08, CL-K09
Several other inputs rest on a single original determination. % CL-K10
A screen that ranks the inputs and states the precision each needs addresses that gap. % CL-G03

% (4) Prior work and the gap: novelty wordings N1 and N2 of lit/novelty.md.
To our knowledge, based on a structured search of the indexed journal and preprint literature (Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, arXiv, OpenAIRE and Crossref, \NumNoveltySearchMonth; patent literature not searched), no previous study has evaluated, for an aluminium current collector in the alkaline aqueous-slurry regime, the combined thermodynamic chain of wet adhesion of a catechol or polysaccharide template to the native oxide, \ce{Ca^2+} sequestration by that template, templated \ce{CaCO3} nucleation on it, and the stability of the resulting film. % CL-V01
Each link has precedent on its own: catechol adsorption on alumina in water has been simulated\cite{yeh2015molecular,yeh2019molecular,shrestha2025dft}; polydopamine enriches interfaces with \ce{Ca^2+} and templates calcium minerals, including continuous \ce{CaCO3} films, on other substrates\cite{ryu2010musselinspired,li2017biomimetic,ko2017natureinspired} and stabilises amorphous \ce{CaCO3}\cite{wang2013amorphous}; and aluminium corrosion in aqueous cathode slurries is mitigated by pH control, carbon coating, phosphate buffering or acidic binders\cite{li2016effect,doberdo2014enabling,maffre2025buffer,soeda2015alginic}. % CL-V02
What has not been reported, within the searched literature, is the evaluation of these links as one chain for this interface and regime. % CL-V01

% (5) Contrary evidence: lit/contrary.md B1 and B10.
Two findings cut against the premise. % CL-E01, CL-E02
Calcite scale precipitated onto aluminium alloys from hot brines was porous and accompanied more alloy corrosion than a non-scaling brine, with attack beneath the deposit\cite{osoriocelestino2020influence}. % CL-E01
An electrodeposited polydopamine coating reduced \ce{CaCO3} deposition relative to bare steel\cite{yu2023study}, the opposite of what templated nucleation needs; the sign of the template's effect is therefore open, not a premise. % CL-E02
The initial finding concerns a porous scale formed in the bulk and settled on the metal, not a coherent film nucleated on a template; that distinction is central to what follows and must be argued from the nucleation and conformality links, not assumed. % CL-E03

% (6) What this paper does and does not do.
This paper is a thermodynamic screen of the chain with literature-bounded inputs. % CL-P03
It writes the four links so that each interfacial free energy enters once (Section~\ref{sec:model}), bounds every input by fetched literature values (Section~\ref{sec:methods}), ranks the inputs by their share of each link's variance, reports the retrodiction of literature cases as it came out and treats the aqueous phase diagram as a thermodynamic proxy (Sections~\ref{sec:methods} and~\ref{sec:results}). % CL-R05, CL-B01, CL-G01, CL-T01, CL-X01
For each governing quantity it states the accuracy an ab initio value must reach to move the outcome across a decision criterion (Section~\ref{sec:results}). % CL-P03
